## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**China beats US to commercial BCI approval with NEO chip** — 
Neuracle Technology's coin-sized NEO implant received regulatory clearance from China's National Medical Products Administration in March and is now available commercially for treating paralysis from spinal cord injuries, marking the first invasive brain-computer interface approved for market use beyond clinical trials worldwide
. 
The device enables people to control a soft robotic glove through thought alone, restoring basic hand movements, and is intended for adults aged 18 to 60 who have partial paralysis but retain some upper arm function, with the system integrated into China's public health insurance
. 
NEO places its eight electrodes on the dura mater — the brain's protective membrane — rather than penetrating the cortex itself
, a design choice that 
faces fewer regulatory constraints because it presents a lower risk of hemorrhage, glial scarring, and long-term signal degradation
. [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/01/1138133/china-world-first-brain-chip/) / [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/china-approves-brain-implant-paralysis-treatment-1801336)

**First new US sunscreen active in 25 years approved** — 
dsm-firmenich received FDA approval for bemotrizinol (brand name PARSOL Shield) for the U.S. market, making it the first new sunscreen active ingredient approved in the United States in more than 25 years under modernized GRASE (Generally Recognized as Safe and Effective) and MUsT (Maximal Usage Trial) evaluation guidelines
. 
The approval marks a major milestone for sunscreen innovation and public skin health in the United States, helping expand access to next-generation UV protection while opening new possibilities for formulators across the industry
. [Globe Newswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/10/3309591/0/en/dsm-firmenich-receives-fda-approval-on-parsol-shield-a-new-sun-protection-ingredient-for-the-u-s.html)

## 🤖 AI

**Great American AI Act discussion draft released** — 
On June 4, 2026, Representatives Jay Obernolte (R-CA) and Lori Trahan (D-MA) released a discussion draft of the Great American Artificial Intelligence Act of 2026 (GAAIA), bipartisan legislation that could create the first comprehensive federal framework for governing AI in the United States
. 
The legislation requires frontier AI model developers to disclose information about those models, obtain third-party audits through designated Independent Verification Organizations (IVOs), and refrain from retaliating against whistleblowers, with these requirements drawing heavily from recently enacted frontier model laws in California, New York, and Illinois
. 
Perhaps the most consequential element of the draft is its three-year preemption of state laws "specifically regulating the development of" any AI model
. [McDonald Hopkins](https://www.mcdonaldhopkins.com/insights/news/the-great-american-ai-act-what-businesses-need-to-know)

## ⚡ AI Productivity

**Microsoft cancels Claude Code licenses internally, pushes GitHub Copilot** —
