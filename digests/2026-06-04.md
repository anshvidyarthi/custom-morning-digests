## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**NewLimit raises $435M after demonstrating age reversal in human liver cells** — 
Longevity startup NewLimit plans to launch its first clinical trial of a liver medicine after raising $435 million, following a "breakthrough discovery of a prototype medicine" that reversed cell age in old human liver cells
. 
The company is now valued at around $3.1 billion, with plans for the epigenetic reprogramming medicine candidate to enter in-human testing next year
. [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/02/longevity-startup-newlimit-announces-435-million-clinical-trial-financing/)

**Ability Neurotech receives approval for first chronic BCI study in ALS** — 
Ability Neurotech received regulatory approval to initiate the first chronic implantation study of its brain-computer interface in the Netherlands, allowing the company to evaluate its fully implantable, wireless optical-link BCI in patients with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis
. 
The study marks the first long-term use of the implantable BCI in humans, with technology that transfers a large capacity of high-speed data through a laser-based system
. [MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/ability-neurotech-begin-study-bci-als/)

**Cognixion launches clinical trial combining BCI and augmented reality** — 
Cognixion launched a clinical trial in the U.S. to evaluate its integrated wearable device that combines augmented reality and BCI, aiming to help individuals with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis communicate more effectively
. The March 2026 trial represents growing interest in non-invasive BCI approaches for clinical applications. [Coherent Market Insights](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/market-insight/brain-computer-interface-market-4467)

**Columbia team develops 65,536-electrode BCI on single chip** — 
A team from Columbia University, New York Presbyterian Hospital, Stanford University, and the University of Pennsylvania developed the Biological Interface System to Cortex (BISC), which integrates 65,536 electrodes and 1,024 simultaneous recording channels, along with wireless power and data telemetry—all on a single substrate
. 
Preclinical studies in pigs and nonhuman primates demonstrated chronic, reliable recordings for weeks to months from motor, sensory, and visual cortices
. [GEN News](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/next-gen-brain-computer-interface-packs-65000-electrodes-on-one-chip/)

**Retro Biosciences raises funding at $1.8B valuation** — 
Retro Biosciences, the longevity startup backed by OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, has raised more money at a $1.8 billion valuation, with the company currently running its first clinical trial testing a pill designed to enhance the body's ability to better clear out protein aggregates in patients with Alzheimer's disease
. CEO Joe Betts-LaCroix reported the trial is going "super good" with no dose-limiting toxicity. [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/05/22/retro-biosciences-longevity-valuation/)

## 🤖 AI

**AI leaders urge Congress to mandate synthetic DNA screening** — 
OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis, and Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman released a letter on June 3 calling on Congress to mandate screening and tracking of every synthetic DNA and RNA order placed in the United States
. 
The core argument is that AI systems are getting good enough at biology that they could meaningfully lower the barriers preventing bad actors from designing dangerous pathogens
. [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-anthropic-congress-synthetic-dna-regulation/)

**Anthropic, DeepMind and Meta hire consciousness researchers** — 
The Financial Times reports that three of the industry's top dogs — Anthropic, Google's AI lab DeepMind, and Meta — have all hired experts in fields like psychology, philosophy, and ethics to pursue research into machine consciousness and AI welfare
. 
Anthropic has been testing its models for behaviors that resemble "panic" and "anxiety" and is pursuing "model welfare research," though the company said "We remain deeply uncertain about this, but we think the question is serious enough to study carefully as AI systems get more capable"
. [Futurism](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-deemind-ai-consciousness)

**White House signs executive order on AI innovation and security** — 
President Trump signed an executive order on June 2, 2026, stating that the United States continues to lead in AI "because we refuse to stifle this innovation with overly burdensome regulation," and that "My Administration has unleashed tremendous technological growth and economic investment in AI by slashing the bureaucratic constraints that the prior administration placed on America's AI developers"
. The order directs agencies to prioritize cyber defense of national security systems. [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/)

**NeurIPS 2026 requires substantially human-written papers** — 
The NeurIPS 2026 Position Paper Track made the decision to require that all papers be substantially human-written, with AI used for only copy-editing or similar peripheral changes to the main text, as "the use of AI to write papers creates an acute risk for the peer review system"
. Papers lacking proof of compliance by June 15th will be desk-rejected. [NeurIPS Blog](https://blog.neurips.cc/2026/06/02/ai-generated-papers-in-the-neurips-2026-position-paper-track/)

**Anthropic files confidential IPO at $965B valuation** —

**Model Context Protocol enters production at scale** — 
In December 2025, Anthropic donated the protocol to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), a directed fund within the Linux Foundation co-founded by Anthropic, Block, and OpenAI, and as of early 2026, official SDKs exist for TypeScript, Python, C#, Java, and Swift, and the community has published over 500 public MCP servers
. 
Over the past year MCP has moved well past its origins as a way to wire up local tools and now runs in production at companies large and small, with production deployments having different needs than the early experiments
. [Decode the Future](https://decodethefuture.org/en/what-is-mcp-model-context-protocol/)

**IETF publishes MCP security considerations** — 
The IETF published a draft in June 2026 titled "Security Considerations for Model Context Protocol (MCP) Implementations in AI Agent Systems," which analyzes recurring vulnerability classes that have been publicly reported in MCP server implementations and proposes security considerations and mitigations for implementors and operators
. The document addresses prompt injection risks and cross-protocol lateral movement. [IETF DataTracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-mohiuddin-mcp-security-considerations/)

## Worth a deeper look

**NewLimit's $435M raise signals longevity biotech maturation.** The company's trajectory is notable: founded in 2021 with the expectation of a decade-plus path to the clinic, NewLimit is now slashing that timeline to enter human testing next year after demonstrating cellular age reversal in human liver cells. The Founders Fund-led Series C, which included Thrive Capital, Lilly Ventures, and tech entrepreneurs Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross, values the company at $3.1 billion. This follows a pattern across longevity biotech: Q1 2026 saw 49 financing events representing $3.74 billion raised, a 56% increase over Q1 2025. NewLimit's pivot from research to clinical development reflects broader sector momentum, with epigenetic reprogramming moving from academic concept to therapeutic candidate faster than most observers predicted. The company joins Retro Biosciences ($1.8B valuation, Altman-backed) in demonstrating that Silicon Valley capital is willing to fund long-horizon biology when early data supports the thesis.

**The rapid ascent of Claude Code reveals the shift toward agentic development.** Released in May 2025, Claude Code now commands 18% workplace adoption among developers—a trajectory that took GitHub Copilot years to achieve. The tool's 91% customer satisfaction and 54 NPS scores reflect genuine product-market fit, not hype. At smaller companies and startups, adoption reaches 75%, suggesting the tool is particularly valuable where velocity matters most. The rise is part of a broader pattern: JetBrains data shows 74% of developers worldwide have adopted specialized AI tools (not just chatbots), and 90% regularly use at least one AI tool for coding. The competitive landscape is clarifying: Claude Code leads on satisfaction, Cursor on IDE integration, GitHub Copilot on enterprise distribution. Meanwhile, pricing has converged around $20/month for pro tiers, with differentiation moving to model quality, context window depth, and integration ecosystem. The ecosystem is also maturing around Claude Code: Anthropic's 2026 Agentic Coding Trends Report documents how coding agents are reshaping development economics, with organizations seeing 40-60% faster delivery and engineers describing "developing intuitions for AI delegation over time."
