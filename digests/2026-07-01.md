## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Ionis scores transformational FDA label expansion for Tryngolza** — 
The FDA approved Tryngolza (olezarsen) for severe hypertriglyceridemia, making it the first treatment to demonstrate a risk-reducing effect for acute pancreatitis in this indication
. 
The approval marks Ionis' transition from two rare disease drugs to a product with millions of potential patients
, with 
peak sales projections raised to $3 billion from $2 billion
. [FirstWord Pharma](https://firstwordpharma.com/story/7646674)

**Vistagen's social anxiety treatment crashes in Phase 3** — 
Vistagen reported that PALISADE-4, testing intranasal fasedienol for acute social anxiety treatment, failed to meet primary and secondary endpoints versus placebo on the Subjective Units of Distress Scale
. 
The company's stock plunged more than 70% to close at 22 cents on June 30
. 
Vistagen plans to pivot toward a longer-term treatment pathway, seeking FDA alignment on a single future Phase 3 trial using the Liebowitz Social Anxiety Scale as primary endpoint
. [Vistagen Press Release](https://www.vistagen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vistagen-announces-topline-and-post-hoc-data-palisade-4-phase-3)

**NEJM retracts Amgen's pivotal Tavneos study amid data manipulation allegations** — 
The New England Journal of Medicine formally retracted the landmark 2021 ADVOCATE trial that supported global approval of Tavneos (avacopan) for ANCA-associated vasculitis
. 
An FDA investigation revealed that primary endpoint assessments for nine patients were altered long after trial protocols should have prohibited modifications
. 
Two study authors requested the retraction after learning results were changed post-database lock while some researchers had been unblinded
. 
Tavneos generated $459 million last year
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/amgen-drug-tavneos-main-study-is-retracted-from-medical-journal)

**BioCryst shutters internal discovery, closes Birmingham facility** — 
BioCryst announced it will discontinue all internal discovery programs and close its Discovery Center of Excellence in Birmingham, Alabama by year-end 2026, pivoting to external innovation as a more capital-efficient path
. 
The company reduced its 2026 non-GAAP operating expense guidance to $420–440 million from $450–470 million
. 
BioCryst will focus on two clinical-stage drugs: navenibart for hereditary angioedema with Phase 3 data expected Q3 2027, and BCX17725 for Netherton syndrome with proof-of-concept data by year-end 2026
. [Endpoints News](https://endpoints.news/biocryst-ends-internal-discovery-programs-will-close-alabama-research-facility/)

**Pharma M&A boom accelerates to record pace** — 
In the first six months of 2026, there have been 33 biotech acquisitions valued at $1 billion or more, with pharmaceutical companies spending around $134 billion—blowing past 2025's full-year total of $112 billion
. 
Lilly led with more than $25 billion across 10 transactions, representing over half the spending by top 12 pharmas
. 
Sun Pharma's $12.6 billion acquisition of Organon was the largest deal of the half, followed by AbbVie's $10.9 billion buyout of Apogee Therapeutics
. [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/22/pharma-biotech-ma-boom-2026-deals-total-123-billion/)

**Motif Neurotech wins FDA approval for depression BCI trial** — 
Motif Neurotech received FDA approval to begin the first clinical trial of its therapeutic brain-computer interface for treatment-resistant depression, a significant milestone for the nearly 3 million Americans living with the condition
. 
The Digitally programmable Over-brain Therapeutic (DOT) device is a small, wirelessly powered implantable device designed to deliver electrical stimulation to brain circuits linked to depression
. 
Motif obtained investigational device exemption just four years after founding—a record timeline for a BCI company—and will enroll eligible adults at Baylor, Mass General Brigham, Emory, UT Health Houston, and other institutions
. [Rice News](https://news.rice.edu/news/2026/brain-computer-interface-based-rice-research-wins-fda-approval-first-clinical-trial)

**AbbVie closes $10.9B Apogee deal to deepen immunology portfolio** — 
AbbVie and Apogee Therapeutics announced a definitive agreement under which AbbVie will acquire Apogee and its diverse pipeline of clinical-stage candidates in inflammatory and immunological diseases, including atopic dermatitis and asthma
. 
Under the terms, AbbVie will acquire all outstanding Apogee shares for $135.11 per share in cash, valuing the company at $10.9 billion in total equity
. 
Apogee's lead asset is zumilokibart (APG777), a late-stage, half-life extended monoclonal antibody targeting IL-13
. [AbbVie Investor Relations](https://news.abbvie.com/2026-06-22-AbbVie-to-Acquire-Apogee-Therapeutics,-Deepening-Immunology-Portfolio)

**Deerfield backs U.S. newco for Metis autoimmune trispecific TCE** — 
A U.S. biotech founded by Deerfield Management licensed exclusive rights to Metis Techbio's preclinical trispecific T-cell engager for autoimmune disorders in a potential $1.62 billion deal
. The transaction reflects growing interest in applying T-cell engager platforms beyond oncology into autoimmune indications. [BioWorld](https://www.bioworld.com/topics/84-bioworld)

## Worth a deeper look

**FDA rejects Unicycive and Sobi filings over manufacturing deficiencies** — 
The FDA issued complete response letters to both Unicycive and Swedish Orphan Biovitrum over contract manufacturing deficiencies
, highlighting continued regulatory scrutiny of manufacturing quality across the industry. This marks the second CRL for Unicycive's kidney drug candidate as manufacturing challenges persist across multiple programs. The trend underscores how manufacturing readiness—once considered a late-stage concern—has become a make-or-break approval criterion. Companies are being forced to invest earlier in CMC development and supply chain resilience, or risk costly delays even after demonstrating clinical efficacy. [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/news)
