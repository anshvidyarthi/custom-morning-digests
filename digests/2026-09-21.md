## 🤖 AI

**Trump Creates "AI Force" and Plans AI Czar Appointment** — 
President Donald Trump announced Saturday that he will appoint an AI czar and create an AI task force, modeled on Space Force, amid growing concerns about the rapidly developing technology
. 
Trump added that "only High I.Q. individuals need apply" to the new AI czar role, though he did not name an appointee
. 
Despite calling safety warnings a "hoax," Trump emphasized maintaining U.S. leadership over China in AI development
. [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/white-house/artificial-intelligence-task-force-czar-technology-trump-rcna598688)

**ChatGPT Desktop Browser Adds Chrome Extension Support** — 
OpenAI added Chrome extension support to the browser inside its ChatGPT desktop app on September 18, letting users install and pin extensions like 1Password without switching windows
. 
The release walls the ChatGPT agent off from extension DOM and data, leaving extensions available to humans while keeping them outside the agent's control
. 
Enterprise admins can disable the browser, restrict sites, block credential imports and enforce policies users can't override
. [RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/openai-chatgpt-desktop-browser-chrome-extensions)

**Antitrust Lawsuit Targets Major AI Labs Over Alleged Development Slowdown** — 
Four ChatGPT/Claude/Grok/Gemini subscribers filed a proposed nationwide antitrust class action on September 13 accusing Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI, and Google of violating Sherman Act Section 1 by coordinating a slowdown of AI development, framed around Dario Amodei's September 12 "Pace the Frontier" essay
. 
Lead attorney Nick Rowley said "the antitrust laws do not permit competitors to decide among themselves that competition is too dangerous"
. [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

## Worth a deeper look

**The Benchmark Saturation Problem** — While no new frontier model releases were recorded in the seven days ending September 19, the AI community is grappling with what comes after benchmark saturation. 
MMLU was the gold standard for AI evaluation from 2020-2023, but in 2026 it's been saturated with top AI models scoring 50-60% on ARC-AGI (while humans score 84%), which represents the most honest signal of where AI's reasoning capabilities genuinely are
. 
MMLU is saturating above 90% and less useful for differentiating frontier models, while GPQA Diamond is now one of the best discriminators with scores ranging widely from 40-85%
. The shift reflects the industry's search for evaluation frameworks that can't be gamed through memorization, pushing toward tests like ARC-AGI that measure genuine reasoning on novel tasks not present in training data.
