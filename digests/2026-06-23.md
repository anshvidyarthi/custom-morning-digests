## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech


**AbbVie closes $10.9B acquisition of Apogee Therapeutics** — The deal marks 2026's second-largest biotech buyout and AbbVie's biggest since its $63 billion Allergan takeover in 2019.
 
The acquisition gives AbbVie control of a late-phase eczema drug candidate that could pose a threat to Eli Lilly, Regeneron and Sanofi in the competitive dermatology market.
 [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/)


**HHS debuts expedited IND pilot to accelerate clinical trials** — Among the health department's efforts is an expedited investigational new drug pilot program that would leverage collaborations with U.S. research institutions to reduce early trial timelines by as much as 12 months.
 The initiative is part of an "unprecedented" effort to restore American leadership in clinical trials. [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/)


**Nuvectis, Haisco ink up to $1.4B deal for two late-stage programs** — China's Haisco Pharmaceutical continues to wield deals, this time out-licensing rights to two late-stage programs to New Jersey biotech Nuvectis.
 The agreement represents continued momentum in cross-Pacific biopharma partnerships. [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/)


**Sofinnova, Sanofi alum unveil Bionyra with $165M** — Formed by Sofinnova Partners and former Sanofi executive Frédéric Marrache, Bionyra Pharma emerged from stealth Monday with an initial focus in inflammatory bowel disease and atopic dermatitis.
 The company brings next-generation immunology assets to the competitive autoimmune disease space. [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/)


**BIO 2026 kicks off in San Diego for industry's 50th anniversary** — The Biotechnology Innovation Organization (BIO) International Convention marks a special year, returning to San Diego and California, the state where biotech started 50 years ago. The world's largest gathering of the biotech industry expects 20,000 attendees and 70,000 scheduled meetings through the BIO Partnering platform.
 
The conference begins with the second-annual AI Summit featuring seven marquee panels, with a total of 20 sessions throughout the week examining various AI use cases. "AI systems will likely have a central role in over the next several years in almost every component of the biotech ecosystem," said Joe Franklin, BIO Chief Legal and Policy Officer.
 [Bio.News](https://bio.news/latest-news/bio-2026-begins-in-san-diego-marking-50-years-of-biotech-innovation/)

## Worth a deeper look

**Biotech IPO market shows structural shift toward larger, later-stage deals** — 
Biopharma companies collectively banked $1.7 billion in IPOs in the first quarter of 2026, the most of any quarter since 2021. Three companies raised more than $300 million, a benchmark rarely seen since the sector's peak in 2021. Biotechs have raised a median of $287.5 million so far in 2026, more than double what was seen during January and March of last year and the highest of any quarter since 2021.
 
The biotechs that have managed to price an offering continue to fit a specific mold: all but one had drugs in mid- or late-stage clinical testing, all previously raised large amounts of venture funding, and all were developing drugs in hot areas of research such as autoimmune conditions or cancer. Offerings remain largely shut to companies not meeting this criteria
, with no preclinical companies going public since 2024. This pattern suggests the biotech financing environment has bifurcated into a "quality tier" accessible only to late-stage, well-capitalized companies addressing validated therapeutic areas, while earlier-stage innovation faces continued funding challenges. [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/biotech-ipo-performance-q1-2026/815879/)
