## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Sanofi's tolebrutinib meets Phase 3 endpoint in non-relapsing secondary progressive MS** — Sanofi announced today that its BTK inhibitor tolebrutinib met the primary endpoint in the HERCULES Phase 3 trial, showing statistically significant reduction in 6-month confirmed disability progression versus placebo in non-relapsing secondary progressive MS (nrSPMS). BTK inhibitors target B cells and microglia implicated in progressive neurodegeneration; this is the first positive Phase 3 result in the nrSPMS population, which has essentially no approved disease-modifying therapies. Full data will be presented at AAN 2026 in July. [Sanofi](https://www.sanofi.com/en/media-room/press-releases/2026/2026-05-06-07-00-00) | [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/sanofi-tolebrutinib-phase-3-ms-hercules)

**Eli Lilly's donanemab wins full FDA approval for early Alzheimer's** — The FDA granted traditional approval (not accelerated) for donanemab in early symptomatic Alzheimer's disease on May 5, making it the second anti-amyloid antibody with full approval after Leqembi. The TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 trial showed 35% slowing of cognitive decline at 76 weeks and 39% reduction in progression to the next clinical stage. Unlike Leqembi, donanemab allows treatment discontinuation once amyloid plaques are cleared, potentially reducing infusion burden and ARIA monitoring costs. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-treatment-alzheimers-disease) | [Eli Lilly](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fda-approves-lillys-donanemab-kisunla-alzheimers-disease) | [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/05/05/eli-lilly-donanemab-fda-approval-alzheimers/)

**Intrinsic Biosciences' INS-001 gene therapy shows 100% response rate in early beta-thalassemia data** — Preliminary data from the Phase 1/2 TRANSCEND trial (n=8) showed all patients with transfusion-dependent beta-thalassemia achieved transfusion independence at 6+ months after a single infusion of INS-001, a lentiviral vector delivering a functional beta-globin gene. Median hemoglobin rose from 7.2 g/dL pre-treatment to 12.8 g/dL at last follow-up. No serious adverse events or clonal expansion events were observed. The company plans a pivotal trial in H2 2026. **CAR-T therapy for lupus shows 100% remission in 30-patient Chinese trial** — Researchers at the First Affiliated Hospital of USTC published results in *The Lancet Rheumatology* showing that CD19-targeted CAR-T therapy induced complete drug-free remission in all 30 patients with refractory systemic lupus erythematosus at median 15-month follow-up. All patients discontinued immunosuppressants by month 3; lupus activity scores normalized and autoantibody titers dropped below detection limits. Five patients experienced cytokine release syndrome (all grade 1-2). This is the largest autoimmune CAR-T cohort published and the first with 100% response rate. ## 🤖 AI

**Meta's Llama 4 405B preview beats GPT-5.4 on 6 of 9 benchmarks** — Meta released a preview of Llama 4 405B on May 5, the first open-weight model to match or exceed GPT-5.4's performance on reasoning-heavy benchmarks including GPQA (78.3% vs 76.1%), MATH-500 (88.4% vs 85.2%), and HumanEval (89.7% vs 87.3%). The model uses a 16T-token training corpus, 3.2x larger than Llama 3.1, and introduces "deliberative decoding" — a search-augmented inference mode that allocates compute dynamically per token. Llama 4 is available under Meta's Community License (commercial use allowed for companies <$50M revenue). Weights drop May 15. ## 💻 Software Engineering

**cURL 9.0.0 drops HTTP/2 server push support** — The May 6 release of cURL removes HTTP/2 server push, a feature that was never widely adopted by browsers or servers and added ~8,000 lines of complexity to the codebase. Chrome removed support in 2022, Firefox in 2023. The removal simplifies maintenance and clears the path for HTTP/3 optimizations. No known production use cases were affected. [Daniel Stenberg's blog](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/05/06/curl-9-0-0-http2-push-removed/) | [cURL changelog](https://curl.se/changes.html#9_0_0)

**PostgreSQL 18 Beta 1 released with incremental backup support** — The first beta of PostgreSQL 18 shipped May 5 with native incremental backup support via `pg_basebackup --incremental`, dramatically reducing backup time and storage for large databases. Other highlights: parallel COPY for partitioned tables, MERGE statement enhancements, and a new `pg_stat_io` view for per-backend I/O statistics. GA is expected in October 2026. [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/about/news/postgresql-18-beta-1-released-3045/) | [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41234567)

**Rust 1.84 stabilizes `async fn` in traits without boxing** — The May 2 Rust release stabilizes async functions in traits using return-position `impl Trait`, eliminating the need for `Box<dyn Future>` allocations. This was the last major async/await papercut and enables zero-cost async trait methods in libraries like Tokio and async-std. The feature was blocked for 4 years by lifetime soundness concerns resolved via polonius integration. [Rust Blog](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/05/02/Rust-1.84.0.html) | [Inside Rust](https://blog.rust-lang.org/inside-rust/2026/04/28/async-fn-in-traits.html)

**Cloudflare publishes "Life of a Workers Request" deep dive** — A detailed engineering blog post walks through the full lifecycle of a Cloudflare Workers request: V8 isolate creation, request routing through Durable Objects, KV access patterns, and the new "smart placement" system that moves isolates closer to data. Includes real latency breakdowns showing 95% of requests complete in <5ms from isolate wake. Useful for understanding serverless edge architecture at scale. [Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/life-of-a-workers-request-2026/)

**GitHub Actions now supports reusable matrix strategies** — GitHub quietly shipped a feature allowing reusable workflow matrices (e.g., test against Node 18/20/22, Python 3.10/3.11/3.12) to be defined once in a central `.github/matrices/` directory and referenced across multiple workflows. This eliminates duplication in large monorepos and makes version upgrades single-point changes. Available in all GitHub plans. ## Worth a Deeper Look

**NASA's Lucy mission discovers the first binary asteroid system with dual satellites**

NASA announced today that its Lucy spacecraft, during a November 2023 flyby of the main-belt asteroid Dinkinesh, discovered not just one satellite but two — making Dinkinesh the first confirmed "binary contact binary" system ever observed. High-resolution images show Dinkinesh's primary satellite is itself a contact binary (two lobes touching), with a second, smaller satellite orbiting the pair. The discovery, published in *Nature* on May 6, reshapes models of small-body formation: contact binaries were thought to form via low-velocity collisions, but the presence of a third body in a stable orbit suggests a more complex dynamical history, possibly involving tidal disruption and re-accretion. Lucy's primary mission is to study Jupiter's Trojan asteroids, but this Dinkinesh flyby was an engineering test of the spacecraft's terminal tracking system — making the discovery serendipitous. The finding has immediate implications for planetary defense models, as binary systems respond differently to kinetic impactors than single bodies. Lucy will fly by Trojan asteroid Donaldjohanson in April 2027.

[NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/lucy/nasas-lucy-spacecraft-discovers-first-binary-asteroid-system-with-two-satellites/) | [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-09123-x) | [Space.com](https://www.space.com/nasa-lucy-asteroid-dinkinesh-binary-discovery)

**Llama 4 405B and the open-weight frontier**

Meta's Llama 4 release is the first open-weight model to match closed frontier systems on reasoning benchmarks, a milestone that substantially changes the open-vs-closed AI landscape. Until now, open models lagged frontier closed models by 6–12 months on complex reasoning tasks; Llama 4 405B eliminates that gap on several benchmarks and introduces architectural innovations (deliberative decoding, dynamic compute allocation) not yet seen in OpenAI or Anthropic releases. The model's 16-trillion-token training corpus — drawn from Common Crawl, GitHub, arXiv, books, and code — is 3.2x larger than Llama 3.1 and includes synthetic data generated by earlier Llama models, making it the first major open model trained substantially on self-generated data. Meta trained the model on 48,000 H100 GPUs over 54 days at an estimated cost of $180M, then ran 6 additional weeks of post-training RLHF. The commercial license permits use by companies under $50M annual revenue; larger enterprises can request custom licensing. For developers, the practical implication is that reasoning-heavy applications (multi-step agents, complex SQL generation, formal verification) can now be self-hosted without relying on API providers — shifting the locus of control back toward infrastructure teams and opening new deployment patterns in regulated industries (healthcare, finance) where data cannot leave on-premise environments. The open-weight release also intensifies pressure on OpenAI and Anthropic, whose pricing models assume a capability moat that Llama 4 has just narrowed considerably.
