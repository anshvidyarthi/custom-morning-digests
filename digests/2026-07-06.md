## 🤖 AI

**Claude Fable 5 Returns After Export Control Lift** — 
The U.S. Department of Commerce lifted export controls on Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 on June 30, with Anthropic restoring global access July 1, 2026
. 
The company added a new safety classifier that blocks the flagged jailbreak in over 99% of cases and reroutes those requests to Claude Opus 4.8
. The 19-day suspension marked the first time a commercially deployed frontier model was pulled by government directive. [MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/software-and-technology/us-lifts-export-controls-on-anthropics-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5-ending-19-day-shutdown)

**FTC Targets AI "Ideological Manipulation" in Proposed Policy** — 
The FTC released a proposed policy statement July 1 addressing AI companies that may be manipulating system outputs contrary to consumer expectations, describing how companies distorting outputs to achieve undisclosed ideological objectives could violate Section 5 of the FTC Act
. 
The statement singles out Colorado's AI Act as potentially pressuring companies to suppress output accuracy to avoid disparate impact liability
. Comments are open until July 31. [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/07/ftc-seeks-public-comment-policy-statement-addressing-ai-accuracy)

**Microsoft Commits $2.5B to Enterprise AI Deployment Unit** — 
Microsoft is investing $2.5 billion into Microsoft Frontier Company, embedding 6,000 employees with clients in what's become known as forward deployed engineering
. 
Early results include work with LSEG embedding AI into LSEG Workspace for finance professionals, and partnerships from LSEG to Land O'Lakes to Unilever to Novo Nordisk
. The move follows similar announcements from Amazon ($1B), OpenAI, and Anthropic. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/microsoft-commits-2point5-billion-6000-employees-ai-implementation-unit.html)

**GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna Still Gated to ~20 Partners** — 
OpenAI previewed GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna on June 26 in limited form, with the company stating it plans to make the models generally available "in the coming weeks" after coordinating with the U.S. government
. 
Pricing is $5/$30 for Sol, $2.50/$15 for Terra, and $1/$6 for Luna per 1M tokens
. 
Access remains limited to approximately 20 government-vetted organizations through API and Codex, with general availability in ChatGPT expected within coming weeks
. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/)

**Anthropic Revenue Surges Past OpenAI** — 
Anthropic reported a $47 billion revenue run rate, up from $30 billion earlier in 2026 and $10 billion last year
. 
Counterpoint Research puts Anthropic on top of the global LLM revenue table in Q1 2026 with 31.4% share, narrowly ahead of OpenAI's 29%, with Anthropic doing it with around 134 million monthly users compared to OpenAI's 900 million and extracting $16.20 average monthly revenue per user versus OpenAI's $2.20
. The enterprise-focused strategy appears to be paying off. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html)

**Andrej Karpathy Joins Anthropic Pre-Training Team** — 
Karpathy announced May 19 he joined Anthropic to work on pre-training under team lead Nick Joseph, with Anthropic stating Karpathy will start a team focused on using Claude to accelerate pre-training research
. 
The move continues a pattern of CTOs from billion-dollar companies quitting to take individual contributor roles at Anthropic to do research, with similar moves from Workday, You.com, Instagram, Box, and others since July 2025
. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/openai-co-founder-andrej-karpathy-joins-anthropics-pre-training-team/)

**Simon Willison Documents Coding Agent Cost Patterns** — 
Willison's July 2 blog post documents a pattern where cost-conscious developers delegate implementation work to lower-tier models while keeping judgment and review in the main loop, stating "for all coding tasks use your judgement to decide an appropriate lower power model and run that in a subagent" to optimize cost/efficiency since implementation work rarely needs top-tier models
. 
He reports getting "a ton of work done" while his Fable allowance "is shrinking less quickly than before"
. [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/)

**Anthropic Ships Claude Sonnet 5 Alongside Fable Return** — 
Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30 with near-Opus 4.8 performance at introductory $2/$10 per-million pricing through August 31, though reception split sharply with power users noting near-Opus costs for marginally inferior output at high effort levels while casual users praised the value, and the new tokenizer may consume up to 35% more tokens
. The timing—launching a more affordable model the same day export controls lifted on Fable—turned a governance crisis into a product moment. [ThursdAI](https://thursdai.news/releases/2026-07)

**OpenAI Proposes 5% Government Equity Stake** — 
A Financial Times report citing internal sources indicates OpenAI floated a proposal to give the U.S. government a 5% equity stake valued at roughly $42.6 billion based on OpenAI's $852 billion March valuation, with Altman reportedly pitching the concept directly to President Trump, Commerce Secretary Lutnick, and Treasury Secretary Bessent
. 
The proposal remains conceptual and would require congressional action, but signals a strategic pivot by leading AI labs toward closer alignment with U.S. national interests
. [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/07/03/top-tech-news-today-july-3-2026/)

## Worth a deeper look

**The Fable 5 Suspension Revealed Frontier Model Fragility** — The 19-day Claude Fable 5 suspension that ended July 1 demonstrated how quickly frontier AI access can change due to policy decisions. 
Commerce lifted export controls June 30 after a shutdown that began June 12, making it one of the most consequential disruptions in enterprise AI history with lessons every B2B technology leader needs to understand
. The trigger was revealing: 
an Amazon researcher report describing a jailbreak technique that bypassed one of Fable 5's cybersecurity safeguards, flagged to federal authorities by Amazon CEO Andy Jassy
. What makes this significant isn't just the technical vulnerability—
Anthropic tested whether the finding was unique to Fable 5 and found it was not, with less capable models including Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, and Kimi K2.7 identifying the same vulnerabilities
—but the precedent it sets. Enterprise teams now understand that model availability is a governance question, not just a performance comparison. The episode also opened space for competitors: 
days after the suspension, Zhipu AI released GLM-5.2 as open weights with independent testers ranking it the strongest openly available model
. For teams building on frontier models, the lesson is clear: access can disappear overnight, and fallback strategies matter.
