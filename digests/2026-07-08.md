## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Atacicept becomes first BAFF/APRIL dual inhibitor approved for kidney disease** — 
The FDA granted accelerated approval to atacicept (Trutakna) on July 7 for reducing proteinuria in adults with IgA nephropathy, supported by Phase 3 ORIGIN 3 data showing a 46% reduction from baseline and 42% reduction versus placebo at 36 weeks
. 
Atacicept is the first therapy targeting both B-cell activating factor and A Proliferation Inducing Ligand for this indication
. 
The FDA notes that atacicept's effect on long-term kidney function decline has not been established; continued approval is contingent on completing confirmatory trials
. [FDA approval announcement](https://www.conexiant.com/Internal-Medicine/viewarticle?id=a6ca066e-dacf-4d03-83f3-86c704e9b28d&pub=Conexiant)

**Vertex makes $10B bet on endocrinology with Crinetics acquisition** — 
Vertex agreed to acquire Crinetics Pharmaceuticals for $10 billion in cash ($85 per share, a 102% premium), adding recently launched acromegaly drug Palsonify and Phase 3 candidate atumelnant for congenital adrenal hyperplasia
. 
Consensus estimates project Palsonify generating $70 million in 2026, with Vertex claiming blockbuster potential; atumelnant targets the rare genetic disease CAH and Cushing's syndrome
. 
Investors responded cautiously, with Vertex shares ticking down post-announcement amid concerns the deal assumes a bullish outcome even though competition exists in the acromegaly market
. [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/vertex-crinetics-acquire-deal-palsonify-acromegaly/824545/)

**Novartis pursues next-generation ADCs with Myricx Bio deal** — 
Novartis agreed to acquire UK biotech Myricx Bio for $1.1 billion upfront plus up to $400 million in milestones, gaining a novel N-myristoyltransferase inhibitor payload platform for antibody-drug conjugates
. 
Myricx's NMTi approach is designed to deliver differentiated cancer-killing payloads directly to tumor cells to address limitations of commonly used TOPO-1 inhibitors; the company is developing lead assets targeting B7-H3 and HER2 across multiple solid tumor settings
. 
The acquisition would enable Novartis to establish NMTi as a new ADC payload class that could be applied across additional targets and platforms if clinically validated
. [Novartis](https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-agrees-acquire-myricx-bio-advancing-next-generation-antibody-drug-conjugate-innovation-novel-nmti-payload-expanding-options-cancer-patients)

**Merck scraps second Alzheimer's drug under Neuphoria partnership** — 
Merck terminated its Phase 2 study of Alzheimer's drug MK-1167 after an early planned review showed the small-molecule candidate did not meet necessary efficacy criteria to warrant further investigation
. 
The study began enrolling in December 2024 and aimed for 350 participants across multiple countries testing whether adding MK-1167 to standard medicines could improve memory and thinking; it closed with last patients finishing in early June 2026
. 
The company stated no safety concerns were identified during the trial but cited business reasons for the discontinuation
. [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/merck-halts-ph-2-alzheimers-trial-after-neuphoria-small-molecule-underwhelms)

**NIH-funded breakthrough shrinks CRISPR for in-body delivery** — 
NIH-funded researchers identified a naturally occurring enzyme Al3Cas12f small enough to fit into adeno-associated virus vectors for targeted delivery, then engineered an enhanced version that dramatically improved gene-editing performance in human cells
. 
The engineered variant Al3Cas12f RKK improved editing efficiency from less than 10% to more than 80% across tested targets, reaching 90% in a commonly edited genome region
. 
The advance addresses a major limitation in CRISPR technology, potentially enabling broader clinical use for cancer, ALS and other diseases
. [NIH](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/nih-funded-breakthrough-shrinks-crispr-precision-delivery-body)

**Ability Neurotech wins approval for first chronic BCI implant in ALS** — 
Geneva-based Ability Neurotech received Dutch regulatory approval to initiate the first chronic implantation study of its fully implantable, wireless optical-link brain-computer interface in ALS patients, transitioning from intraoperative testing to long-term clinical investigation
. 
The device features electrodes, a thin battery-free implant placed on the skull under closed skin, and a wearable external device that uses laser-based data transfer to extract raw brain recordings at high capacity and speed, translating neural intent into digital action for text generation and communication
. 
The study will be conducted at UMC Utrecht under the INTRECOM consortium
. [MassDevice](https://www.massdevice.com/ability-neurotech-begin-study-bci-als/)

**Genmab's B-cell bispecific meets endpoint in relapsed DLBCL** — 
Genmab announced on June 29 that its Phase 3 EPCORE DLBCL-4 trial evaluating epcoritamab in combination with lenalidomide met its primary endpoint, demonstrating statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in progression-free survival compared with standard-of-care chemoimmunotherapy in patients with relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma
. The CD3xCD20 bispecific antibody continues to accumulate positive data across multiple B-cell malignancies. [Life Science Daily News](https://lifesciencedaily.news/clinical-trials-roundup-03-july-2026/)

**REGENXBIO completes Duchenne gene therapy confirmatory dosing** — 
REGENXBIO completed confirmatory-study dosing for RGX-202, its Duchenne muscular dystrophy gene therapy in the AFFINITY DUCHENNE pivotal program, and plans to initiate a Biologics License Application in Q3 2026 under accelerated approval
. The milestone marks another step in translating AAV-based gene therapy into commercial reality for rare genetic diseases. [LucidQuest Ventures](https://www.lqventures.com/cell-and-gene-therapy-today-july-2-2026-lucidquest/)

**FDA maintains approval pace despite staffing turbulence** — 
The FDA cleared 23 novel drugs by June 2026, the most in the first half since 2023, holding up approval rates despite staff cuts and internal upheaval

Acting Commissioner Kyle Diamantas took over after Commissioner Marty Makary's May 12 exit following mass layoffs, senior departures, and controversial regulatory decisions during his year-long tenure

## Worth a deeper look

**Baby KJ and the personalized CRISPR era** — 
KJ Muldoon was born in August 2024 with carbamoyl-phosphate synthetase 1 (CPS1) deficiency, an ultra-rare metabolic disorder affecting around one in 1.3 million live births that prevents the liver from processing protein and causes toxic ammonia accumulation; without intervention, half of affected infants do not survive their first year. Researchers at Children's Hospital of Philadelphia and Penn Medicine designed and delivered a bespoke base-editing therapy, with the first infusion administered in February 2025 when he was between six and seven months old, making him the first person to receive personalized CRISPR-based gene therapy. As of early 2026, he is walking and showing only mild symptoms
. 
In February 2026, the FDA published draft guidance introducing the "plausible mechanism framework," a pathway designed for individualized genomic therapies targeting ultra-rare diseases that allows a single clinical trial to test a platform technology customized for each patient without requiring separate regulatory applications for every unique genetic variant
. This case represents a pivotal shift from population-scale clinical trials toward N-of-1 medicine enabled by gene editing. [Life Science Daily News](https://lifesciencedaily.news/crispr-gene-editing-2026/)
