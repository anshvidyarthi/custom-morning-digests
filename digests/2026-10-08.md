**Jobless Claims Hold Near Historic Lows in Tight Labor Market** — 
The Labor Department said initial jobless claims ticked down to 197,000 from a revised 199,000 the week before

Claims have remained at a historically low level below 220,000 so far this year

hiring has been sluggish, resulting in a "low-hire, low-fire" job market with unemployment at 4.2%

## Worth a deeper look

**Oil Traders Numb to Trump's Iran Rhetoric—Until Thursday** — For months, crude markets had grown desensitized to President Trump's escalating threats against Iran. 
On October 1, Trump told reporters Iran should sign a ceasefire deal or cease to exist—and crude prices barely moved, a stark contrast to April 1 when similar rhetoric sent prices soaring
. 
Oil traders say they're less focused on rhetoric and instead more reactive to evidence of changes in physical supply or cargo flows, while also decreasing the size of their wagers given the unpredictability of US war strategy
. Yet Thursday's pre-midterm strike report broke through the fatigue: 
The Atlantic reported that officials said preparations signal how eager Trump is to reduce gas prices and show progress before voters head to the polls, sending Brent up more than 4%
. The move underscores that markets still assign a risk premium to actual military planning, even if they've tuned out the bluster. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-trump-oil-reactions/), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-nuclear-donald-trump-vance-rubio-strait-of-hormuz/)
