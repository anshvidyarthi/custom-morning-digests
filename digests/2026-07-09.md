## 📈 Finance & Stock Markets

**Micron raises U.S. investment plan to $250 billion** — 
Micron announced Thursday it will invest more than $250 billion in the U.S. through 2035, up from $200 billion previously announced last June, driven by surging AI memory chip demand
. 
Shares jumped about 8% in early trading, adding to a more than 200% surge so far this year
. 
The company will spend $3 billion strengthening the U.S. semiconductor supply chain, including $500 million to fund GlobalWafers' silicon wafer facility in Texas
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/micron-boosts-us-spending-to-250-billion-amid-memory-demand)

**SK Hynix U.S. listing sees massive oversubscription** — 
SK Hynix is poised to finalize pricing Friday for its Nasdaq debut, with demand running at seven times the available shares in what could be the second-biggest IPO ever
. 
The South Korean memory chipmaker aims to raise $28 billion, the largest foreign listing in history, to expand manufacturing capacity for AI-driven memory chip demand
. 
SK Hynix captures 56.4% of the high-bandwidth memory market according to its SEC filing
. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/sk-hynix-ipo-to-give-us-investors-bigger-bite-of-the-memory-pie-as-shortages-persist-193700352.html)

**AstraZeneca drug failure sends ripples through pharma** — 
AstraZeneca shares fell more than 8% Thursday after its Wainua drug failed to show improvement in cardiovascular mortality and recurrent CV events in a phase 3 trial for heart disease patients, denting the drug's commercial prospects
. 
The stock lost £23.3 billion in market value at the day's low, marking its biggest one-day drop in nine years
. 
Partner Ionis Pharmaceuticals dropped 13-15% while competitor Alnylam surged 17% in premarket trading
. [pharmaphorum](https://pharmaphorum.com/news/az-ionis-hit-unexpected-wainua-trial-failure)

**Apple and Broadcom expand chip partnership to $30 billion** — 
Apple announced Wednesday a new multiyear agreement with Broadcom expected to exceed $30 billion, leading to production of more than 15 billion U.S.-made chips and supporting hundreds of American jobs
. 
Broadcom will invest $1.5 billion to expand and modernize its Fort Collins, Colorado manufacturing facility under the agreement
. 
Broadcom gained 4.8% Wednesday after expanding its agreement with Apple, which rose 0.9%
. [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/07/apple-to-increase-spend-with-broadcom-to-produce-billions-more-us-chips/)

**FOMC minutes reveal hawkish split as oil stokes inflation fears** — 
Fed minutes released Wednesday showed "many" officials assessed the appropriate federal funds rate would be above the current target range by year's end, reflected in nine dots projecting rate hikes
. 
Traders increased expectations for a September rate increase to around 70%, up from 58% the previous day
. 
The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.59% Wednesday, its highest since mid-May, as escalating Middle East tensions drove oil prices sharply higher and fueled concerns over renewed inflationary pressures
. [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcminutes20260617.htm)

**Amazon bond sale meets tepid demand as AI debt floods market** — 
Amazon's $25 billion bond offering saw orders pare to about $41 billion once banks trimmed final spreads, settling at just 1.6 times the deal's size after peaking at $62 billion
. 
The offering drew the weakest demand with orders at 2.5 times bonds on offer, down from 3.2 times in March, marking the poorest new-issue performance for any hyperscaler since Meta's $30 billion sale in October 2025
. 
Amazon won't issue any more debt this year after raising roughly $54 billion in bonds earlier in 2026 in the U.S. and Europe, plus $10 billion in Canada
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-07/amazon-returns-to-us-bond-market-to-fund-ai-infrastructure-build)

**Markets whipsaw on renewed U.S.-Iran conflict** — 
U.S. stock indices closed mixed Wednesday with the S&P 500 falling 0.3%, the Dow losing 577 points, while the Nasdaq 100 gained 0.3% as President Trump said the ceasefire with Iran was "over" following renewed strikes
. 
International Brent crude futures settled up 5.43% at $78.19 per barrel while WTI futures popped 4.37% to close at $73.52
. 
Minutes from the Fed's June meeting showed some policymakers saw a case for further rate hikes if inflation remains elevated
. [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/stock-market)

**June jobs report misses expectations, unemployment falls** — 
U.S. nonfarm payrolls increased by 57,000 in June, well below the 115,000 consensus forecast and slower than a downwardly revised 129,000 in May
. 
The unemployment rate dropped to 4.2% largely due to labor force participation falling 0.3 percentage point to 61.5%, the lowest since March 2021
. 
Prior months saw significant downward revisions with May cut by 43,000 and April down 31,000, showing labor market growth significantly slower than previously thought
. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/jobs-report-june-2026-.html)

**TeraWulf soars on $19B Anthropic data center lease** — 
TeraWulf executed a 20-year lease agreement with Anthropic at its Kentucky data campus, expected to generate approximately $19 billion of contracted revenue over the initial lease term
. 
The data center will have capacity around 400 megawatts with first power delivery expected in the second half of 2027; TeraWulf shares soared more than 16% in premarket trading
. 
The lease represents more than TeraWulf's entire current market value of around $12 billion in future revenue
. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/06/anthropic-terawulf-data-center-ai.html)

**Semiconductors rebound after Tuesday selloff** — 
A gauge of semiconductor firms climbed 4% Thursday after earlier selling pressure, with SK Hynix's U.S. listing more than seven times oversubscribed underscoring strong investor appetite
. 
Shares of Micron closed down 4.7% Tuesday alongside declines in KLA, Marvell Technology, Broadcom and AMD, with the VanEck Semiconductor ETF falling more than 3%
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-08/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates)

## Worth a deeper look

**Fed policy split deepens amid geopolitical uncertainty** — 
FOMC minutes showed asset prices were affected by multiple factors including Middle East developments, solid economic data, higher inflation readings, and ongoing AI investment, with optimism around a U.S.-Iran memorandum of understanding pushing oil futures and near-term inflation compensation materially lower relative to April levels
. 
Many officials assessed rates would need to rise above the current target range by year's end as reflected in the nine hawkish dots, though many others felt rates would remain within or slightly below the current range, with most remaining open to cuts if inflation dissipates
. 
Market participants generally expected no change at the June meeting, but market- and survey-based measures of expected policy rates moved higher over the intermeeting period
. The unusual lack of forward guidance from Chair Kevin Warsh—who withheld his own rate projection from the dot plot for the first time since its 2012 launch—places outsized importance on Wednesday's minutes release as the committee's primary on-record statement about September rate intentions.
