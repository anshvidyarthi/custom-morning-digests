## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech


**China approves world's first invasive BCI for clinical use** — Dong Hui, a 39-year-old paralyzed from the neck down after a car accident, can now write his name after 11 months of rehabilitation enabled by a brain implant approved in China. The approval represents the first invasive brain-computer interface to receive regulatory clearance, with strong government support expected to accelerate China's push to become a global leader in brain implants.
 [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/01/1138133/china-world-first-brain-chip/)


**Spanish ADC biotech raises $86.6M series B** — Ona Therapeutics closed an $86.6 million series B round to support its antibody-drug conjugate program focused on treatment-resistant tumor biology in breast cancer. The funds will also advance a second colorectal ADC asset toward first-in-human studies, deploying a proprietary patient-driven target discovery platform to identify novel tumor-specific antigens.
 [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-fundraising-tracker-26)


**FDA panel recommends updating COVID-19 vaccines for XFG variant** — An FDA advisory committee recommended Thursday that COVID-19 vaccines for the 2026-2027 season should be updated to target the XFG variant. The agency noted that XFG variants remain "dominant in the U.S. and globally" and that all licensed COVID vaccine manufacturers have indicated they are prepared to produce an XFG vaccine for their 2026 to 2027 formula.
 [Fierce Pharma](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/fierce-pharma-regulatory-tracker-2026)


**AI-driven aging target discovery identifies 29 high-confidence candidates** — A new bioRxiv preprint describes an AI-driven target discovery framework that integrates large-scale multi-omic datasets to prioritize therapeutic targets shared between aging and 12 age-related diseases. The study identified 29 high-confidence and 16 previously unrecognized aging-associated targets, with convergent pathway perturbations characterized by robust upregulation of interferon and inflammatory signaling alongside coordinated downregulation of MYC-driven proliferative programs.
 [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.02.13.705676v1)

## Worth a deeper look


**China's BCI regulatory approval marks global first** — The approval of Dong Hui's brain implant in China represents a watershed moment in neurotechnology. After sustaining spinal cord injuries in a car accident six years earlier that left him paralyzed from the neck down, Dong became one of the first people in China to receive an invasive brain-computer interface through brain surgery in November 2024. Before the implant, Dong could move his arms slightly but wasn't able to use his fingers; after 11 months of rehabilitation enabled by the implant, he can now write. China's strong government support is expected to help accelerate the country's push to become a global leader in brain implants, creating a competitive dynamic with Western BCI efforts from Neuralink, Synchron, and others.
 [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/01/1138133/china-world-first-brain-chip/)


**Project Glasswing scales open-source security scanning** — Anthropic's expansion of Project Glasswing demonstrates the growing practical application of AI in software security. In early April, roughly 50 initial partners had access to Claude Mythos Preview, and they've been deploying the model to scan their codebases for vulnerabilities, finding more than 10,000 high- or critical-severity security flaws. Following several weeks of close collaboration with Project Glasswing partners, the security industry, open-source software maintainers, and the U.S. government, Anthropic is now extending the partnership to approximately 150 new organizations. The company also plans to share vulnerability-finding tools with trusted security teams to strengthen cyberdefenses, representing a shift from keeping such tools proprietary to enabling broader defensive use in the security community.
 [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)
