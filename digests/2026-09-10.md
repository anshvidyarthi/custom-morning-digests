## 📈 Finance & Stock Markets

**Markets sink for fourth straight day as oil and Treasury yields climb** — 
S&P 500 fell 0.59%, Dow lost 0.35%, and Nasdaq dropped 0.97%, with the Russell 2000 down 1.32%
. 
The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.91% and the 30-year yield climbed to 5.35%, both hitting multi-year highs
. 
Oil prices topped $105 per barrel after Iran targeted US Navy warships in the latest escalation in the Middle East conflict
. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-september-10-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-083717303.html)

**August PPI meets estimates but annual rate jumps** — 
Wholesale prices rose 0.4% in August, matching consensus, but the annual rate climbed to 5.4% from July's 4.8%
. 
The data increased bets for a Federal Reserve rate hike next week, with traders now pricing a 70% chance according to the CME FedWatch Tool, up from 62% before the data
. 
Clark Bellin of Bellwether Wealth said the monthly reading was a relief for the Fed, and that a relatively benign CPI report Friday may prompt the Fed to pause on rates this time
. [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-10-2026)

**Cooper Companies plunges 17% after earnings miss, guidance cut** — 
Cooper delivered Q3 non-GAAP EPS of $1.15, surpassing the $1.11 consensus, but revenue of $1.07B barely met the $1.06B expectation, marking only 1.0% growth
. 
CooperVision, the company's largest segment, generated $717.0M in revenue but showed zero year-over-year growth
. 
The company surprised investors by deciding not to sell CooperSurgical following its strategic review, after management had highlighted prospective buyers on the last earnings call
. [Alphastreet](https://news.alphastreet.com/cooper-companies-slides-after-q3-2026-stock-drops-17/)

**Enbridge acquires Tallgrass crude business in $2.55 billion deal** — 
Enbridge entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Tallgrass Energy's crude oil business for approximately $2.55 billion, including 75% of Pony Express Pipeline and 51% of Powder River Gateway
. 
The acquisition is expected to strengthen Enbridge's position in the Bakken, Powder River and Denver-Julesberg basins and provide direct connections to approximately 500,000 barrels per day of new refining capacity
. 
The transaction follows Enbridge's August agreement to acquire Salt Creek Midstream's crude gathering business for $600 million, and the company launched a C$2.6-billion equity offering to help fund both deals
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/canada-s-enbridge-to-buy-tallgrass-oil-business-for-2-6-billion)

**AeroVironment beats Q1 estimates, shares jump on defense demand** — 
The defense drone maker reported adjusted earnings of $0.59 per share on revenue of $480.5M, significantly exceeding Wall Street estimates of $0.30 and $459.9M, and funded backlog reached a record $1.5B, up 37% year-over-year
. 
Shares outperformed after results, with Q1 adj. EPS topping consensus by $0.34, as results were driven by increased product sales and higher service revenue
. 
As of quarter-end, AeroVironment had visibility into 86% of its full-year revenue guidance midpoint, up from 69% at the end of fiscal 2026
. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/aerovironment-q1-fy27-slides-record-backlog-directed-energy-wins-93CH-4894697)

**Fox-Roku merger faces DOJ second request** — 
On September 8, Fox and Roku each received a request for additional information from the DOJ in connection with its review of the $22 billion merger, extending the waiting period under the HSR Act until 30 days after both companies substantially comply
. 
An in-depth review can lengthen the regulatory process by months or years, though Fox said it expects the merger to be completed by the first half of 2027
. 
Fox shares advanced 4.5% Thursday as investors reacted positively to reassuring comments regarding the pending acquisition
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/fox-roku-merger-gets-a-closer-look-by-us-justice-department)

**Oracle and Adobe set to report after close today** — 
Oracle is set to announce fiscal Q1 2027 results after the close with analysts forecasting EPS of $1.74 and revenue of $19.14B, with investors focused on the company's $638B in remaining performance obligations and whether cloud order growth will translate into realized revenue
. 
Oracle shares are struggling this year as investors balk at the massive debt load the company has taken on to build out AI infrastructure, and Thursday's earnings will give Wall Street an indication of how much patience investors have left for such heavy AI spending
. 
Adobe is set to report Q3 earnings with expectations of $6.69B in revenue and $6.08 EPS, as the company faces competitive pressures in the AI space
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/oracle-earnings-to-test-market-s-tolerance-for-ai-spending-risk)

**Friday's CPI report looms as Fed's last inflation input** — 
Headline CPI is expected to be up 0.4% from July to August and 3.4% from the year prior, while core CPI is forecast to rise 0.4% month over month and 2.4% year over year
. 
The August CPI report is the last inflation update the Fed will see before next week's policy meeting
. 
If the August report looks worse than expected, pressure will build on the committee to start raising short-term rates by a quarter point at the September 16 meeting and the two following meetings in October and December
. [Kiplinger](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/economy/cpi-report-august-2026-what-to-expect)

## Worth a deeper look

**The Fed's inflation conundrum deepens ahead of next week's decision** — 
The ongoing US-Iran war and disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz have raised concerns that an energy shock could flow into broader inflation and all but force the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates
. 
Oil prices jumped 4% Thursday with benchmark Brent crude hitting $105 a barrel after the biggest spike in attacks on shipping since the start of the US-Iran war spurred supply disruption concerns, and the European Central Bank raised interest rates for the second time this year hoping to tame an inflation rise, with surging oil and natural gas prices pushing inflation well past 3% across the euro zone
. 
Interventions from Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, including an upsized bond buyback, have done little to bring long-dated yields lower
. The combination of geopolitical risk, persistent inflation, and rising bond yields creates one of the most challenging policy environments in years—and next week's FOMC decision has become a genuine coin flip.
