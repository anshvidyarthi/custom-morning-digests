## ⚛️ Quantum & Deep Tech

**Japan launches Shunkai neutral-atom quantum computer** — 
Japan's National Institutes of Natural Sciences switched on Shunkai, the country's first full-stack neutral-atom quantum computer, with a roadmap to scale from 50 qubits today to 10,000 qubits with fault tolerance by March 2031
. 
The system uses optical tweezers—tightly focused laser beams—to trap neutral atoms in a vacuum chamber and operates at room temperature, unlike superconducting designs that require cryogenic cooling
. [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/technology/quantum/japan-switches-on-its-first-full-stack-room-temperature-quantum-computer-and-scientists-plan-to-scale-it-up-to-10-000-qubits)

**Classiq and IonQ validate QAOA for natural-gas network optimization** — 
Classiq Technologies and Israel Natural Gas Lines (INGL) published research demonstrating Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) workflows to maximize gas throughput, validated on the IonQ Forte-1 quantum computer
. The study, posted to arXiv (2609.00825) on September 23, shows how hybrid quantum-classical optimization can address complex energy infrastructure challenges with real utility operators. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**D-Wave and CGI partner on enterprise quantum optimization** — 
CGI and D-Wave announced a collaboration to integrate D-Wave's quantum annealing hardware into CGI's IT services, targeting quantum optimization solutions for transportation, logistics and retail, with a focus on moving clients from proof-of-concept to full production deployment
. The partnership aims to leverage CGI's global reach and D-Wave's quantum annealing technology to address complex scheduling, routing and supply-chain problems. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**IonQ demonstrates commodity-CPU real-time quantum error correction** — 
IonQ demonstrated a real-time Quantum Error Correction (QEC) decoding pipeline capable of handling large-scale fault-tolerant workloads on a single commodity CPU, achieving near-zero computational backlog delays and validating a core component of IonQ's Walking Cat architecture for multi-thousand physical qubit systems
. The dual-decoder approach eliminates the need for expensive custom hardware, published September 22. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**University of Houston shatters 33-year ambient-pressure superconductivity record** — 
University of Houston physicists achieved a transition temperature of 151 Kelvin (about minus 122 degrees Celsius) under ambient pressure using mercury-based copper-oxide ceramic Hg1223, the highest Tc ever recorded for ambient-pressure superconductors since 1911
. 
The team used pressure quenching—applying intense pressure, cooling to a specific temperature, then rapidly releasing pressure—to "lock in" enhanced superconducting properties
. [Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences](https://phys.org/news/2026-03-ceramic-shatters-longstanding-high-temperature.html)

**TSMC, Samsung commit to ASML High-NA EUV for AI-era chipmaking** — 
TSMC and Samsung committed to using ASML's High NA EUV lithography machines, with Samsung planning to use High NA EUV in 2028 for DRAM memory manufacturing and TSMC expecting adoption in 2030 for advanced chips driven primarily by increasingly complex transistor architectures required for AI applications
. 
ASML reported 10 High-NA EUV systems already running at four customers worldwide, with Intel using the technology in high-volume manufacturing for select Core Ultra Series 3 processors on Intel 18A
. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/tsmc-samsung-asml-high-na-euv-machine-ai-chips.html)

**Intel processes one million High-NA EUV wafers in production** — 
Intel Foundry and ASML disclosed at SPIE Photomask Technology conference that more than one million wafers have been processed using High NA EUV to date, spanning early tool certification, R&D efforts and volume production on select layers for Intel Core Ultra Series 3 processors
. 
Products manufactured on Intel 18A using High NA for select layers continue to deliver performance that meets or exceeds comparable layers patterned using 0.33 NA EUV
. [Intel Foundry](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/intel-foundry/intel-foundry-asml-accelerate-industry-readiness-for-high-na-euv.html)

**Silicon photonics commercialization accelerates with TSMC COUPE ramp** — 
TSMC's COUPE silicon photonics platform entered mass production in 2026, expanding access to silicon photonics manufacturing beyond the largest hyperscale customers, while Ayar Labs closed a USD 500 million Series E funding round at a USD 3.75 billion valuation to support volume production of its TeraPHY optical engine chiplets
. 
Silicon photonics is expected to command more than 50 percent of the optical transceiver market in 2027, with TSMC's compact universal photonic engine offering more than 10 times power efficiency compared with traditional copper wire-based applications
. [Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/biz/archives/2026/09/01/2003863454)

**Global survey finds 90% of quantum firms rely on cross-border supply chains** — 
A global study conducted by international quantum industry consortia found that 90% of quantum companies rely on cross-border supply chains
. The finding underscores the vulnerability of the quantum industry to export controls and geopolitical tensions, particularly for EUV lithography, cryogenic systems and rare isotopes. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

## Worth a deeper look

**ASML and TSMC launch 12-inch photomask initiative for next-generation High-NA EUV** — 
TSMC intends to use ASML's High NA technology in high-volume manufacturing for advanced nodes starting in 2030, and on September 7 ASML and TSMC established an industry-wide collaborative initiative to drive the transition to 12-inch photomasks, aiming to establish a 12-inch mask pilot line by 2031 and enabling 12-inch High-NA lithography systems to enter advanced node production by 2033
. 
While High NA EUV will be adopted for production using current 6-inch masks, the transition to larger 12-inch masks is expected to further increase fab productivity, lower chipmaking costs and remove stitching constraints, benefiting the entire semiconductor industry
. The announcement signals that all three leading-edge foundries—Intel, Samsung and TSMC—have now aligned on High-NA EUV as the critical lithography step for sub-2nm nodes through 2030 and beyond. [ASML](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/tsmc-and-asml-announce-industry-transition-to-large-format-photomasks-for-high-na-euv)
