## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Gut bacteria transplants reverse liver aging in mice** — 
Researchers at Digestive Disease Week 2026 showed that transplanting stored young gut microbes back into the same aged mice produced striking anti-aging effects in the liver, reducing age-related damage
. 
The fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) technique preserved samples from young animals and returned them later
, suggesting microbiome restoration could become a longevity intervention. [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260509210643.htm)

**Microbiome shifts drive intestinal aging, not time alone** — 
Recent advances show age-related physiological changes are shaped by shifts in the gut microbial community rather than chronological aging alone, with declining diversity and loss of short-chain fatty acid-producing taxa weakening the intestinal barrier and contributing to inflammaging
. 
The research highlights host-microbe interactions as a promising target for interventions designed to maintain tissue regeneration and support healthy aging
. [PMC / Inside Precision Medicine](https://www.insideprecisionmedicine.com/topics/translational-research/gut-bacteria-may-hold-the-key-to-reversing-intestinal-aging/)

**Low-dose antibiotic reprograms gut bacteria as anti-aging factories** — 
Low doses of cephaloridine activated gene expression in gut bacteria involved in making colanic acids, leading to higher good cholesterol, lower bad cholesterol in male mice, and reduced insulin levels in females
. 
When taken orally, cephaloridine isn't absorbed into the bloodstream, allowing it to influence the gut microbiome without affecting the rest of the body
. [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/01/260131085024.htm)

## Worth a deeper look

**The end of flat-rate AI coding subscriptions** — The shift from flat monthly fees to usage-based billing represents a fundamental restructuring of the AI tools market. 
GitHub's Chief Product Officer Mario Rodriguez explained that a quick chat question and an hours-long automated coding session can cost the user the same amount under the old model; GitHub has been absorbing most of the growing inference cost, but the Premium requests model is no longer sustainable
. 
A "Pro at ~$20 / power tier at ~$200" structure has emerged across multiple tools (Cursor, Windsurf, Claude Code); even with different metering systems, the market is landing in the same place—a $20/month seat can't fund unlimited agentic compute anymore
. The convergence suggests vendors have crossed an economic threshold where subsidizing heavy users through flat pricing no longer works at scale. [Wilico](https://wilico.co.jp/en/blog/end-of-flat-rate-ai-github-copilot-llm-billing-shift)

**Gut microbiome as the reversible aging clock** — The mounting evidence linking gut bacteria composition to aging markers opens a striking possibility: aging may be more reversible than previously thought. 
Recent advances show that age-related physiological changes are shaped by shifts in the gut microbial community rather than by time alone; aging is frequently accompanied by a decline in microbial diversity and loss of short-chain fatty acid-producing taxa, changes that weaken the intestinal barrier and contribute to inflammaging, intersecting with immune and metabolic pathways linked to immunosenescence, cellular senescence, and mitochondrial function
. 
Microbial ecosystems enriched with butyrate-producing and polyamine-generating species have been associated with more stable epithelial integrity, improved metabolic flexibility, and balanced immune activity; emerging findings indicate the gut microbiota communicates with peripheral organs through the gut-skin, gut-muscle, and gut-brain axes
. The therapeutic implication is profound: rather than treating aging as an irreversible process, interventions targeting the microbiome—through FMT, dietary modulation, probiotics, or postbiotics—could restore biological function years after decline begins. [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12828327/)
