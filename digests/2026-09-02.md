## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech


**GSK advances bivalent mRNA flu vaccine to Phase 3 after outperforming licensed shots** — GSK will launch Phase 3 trials in September 2026 for its investigational mRNA seasonal influenza vaccine that targets both hemagglutinin and neuraminidase, after the candidate generated stronger immune responses against every influenza strain tested than currently licensed flu vaccines in Phase 2 while remaining generally well tolerated.
 
The FDA granted the candidate Fast Track designation in July 2026.
 [Contagion Live](https://www.contagionlive.com/view/gsk-s-mrna-influenza-vaccine-moves-to-phase-3-trial)


**Novartis' remibrutinib meets Phase 3 primary endpoint in relapsing multiple sclerosis** — Novartis reported that its oral Bruton's tyrosine kinase (BTK) inhibitor remibrutinib met the primary endpoint in two Phase 3 trials, REMODEL-1 and REMODEL-2, showing a statistically significant reduction in annualized relapse rate compared to teriflunomide in adults with relapsing multiple sclerosis.
 
Both trials also showed remibrutinib was superior across key secondary endpoints including MRI lesion reductions, and the pooled analysis indicated a nominally statistically significant result for six-month confirmed disability progression.
 
Novartis is planning to file for remibrutinib's expanded approval in relapsing multiple sclerosis by next year, and emphasized that remibrutinib showed no concerning liver signals in the REMODEL studies.
 [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/drug-development/novartis-rhapsido-cuts-ms-progression-in-phase-3-trial-teeing-up-roche-battle)


**Novartis and Bristol Myers pause autoimmune CAR-T trials after patient deaths** — Novartis and Bristol Myers Squibb paused multiple trials evaluating their respective cell therapy programs against autoimmune conditions after Novartis found three cases of immune effector cell-associated hemophagocytic syndrome with fatal outcomes, and Bristol Myers observed "transient and reversible inflammatory events."
 
Novartis suspended eight studies of rapcabtagene autoleucel (rap-cel) including Phase 2 AUTOGRAPH trials in systemic lupus erythematosus, systemic sclerosis, idiopathic inflammatory myopathies, and Phase 1/2 trials in rheumatoid arthritis, ANCA-associated vasculitis, myasthenia gravis, and multiple sclerosis.
 [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/novartis-bristol-myers-autoimmune-cell-therapy-trial-halt/829218/)


**BioNTech and Genentech terminate colorectal cancer vaccine trial after survival imbalance** — BioNTech terminated the Phase 2 BNT122-01 trial evaluating autogene cevumeran as adjuvant monotherapy in patients with circulating tumor DNA-positive, surgically resected Stage II (high risk) or Stage III colorectal cancer following a Data Safety Monitoring Board recommendation that identified a numerical imbalance in overall survival between treatment arms.
 
Genentech confirmed there were more deaths in the vaccine arm in this specific patient population.
 
Analysts said the failure is a negative signal for mRNA vaccines in "cold" tumors historically resistant to immunotherapy, like colorectal and pancreatic cancers, unlike "hot" tumors such as melanoma where Moderna succeeded.
 [BioPharm International](https://www.biopharminternational.com/view/biontech-halts-phase-2-trial-of-individualized-mrna-cancer-vaccine-autogene-cevumeran-in-colorectal-cancer)


**Cleveland Clinic's CRISPR cholesterol therapy shows sustained benefit at one year** — After 12 months, patients treated with the highest dose of CRISPR Therapeutics' investigational CRISPR-Cas9 therapy CTX310 saw a 52.5% lowering from baseline in LDL cholesterol and a 47.8% reduction in triglycerides, with no serious adverse events related to the therapy during the one-year follow-up.
 
Study participants were monitored for one year following the trial, with additional long-term safety follow-up planned for the next 15 years, as recommended by the FDA for all gene-editing therapies.
 [Cleveland Clinic](https://newsroom.clevelandclinic.org/2026/08/28/cleveland-clinic-first-in-human-trial-of-crispr-gene-editing-therapy-shown-to-safely-and-continuously-lower-cholesterol-and-triglycerides-after-one-year)


**UniQure on track for Q3 2026 Huntington's disease gene therapy filing after FDA reversal** — UniQure intends to submit a Biologics License Application in the third quarter of 2026 for AMT-130 in Huntington's disease after the FDA agreed that 3-year data from Phase I/II studies comparing treatment to a propensity score-matched external control could serve as the primary basis for accelerated approval.
 
In March 2026, the FDA had called for a larger, randomized, double-blind trial with sham surgery; the agency is now considering a concurrent standard-of-care control group instead of sham surgery.
 [uniQure](https://uniqure.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/uniqure-announces-plan-bla-submission-amt-130-huntingtons)


**Lilly acquires Merida for up to $2.88 billion in autoimmune expansion** — Eli Lilly plans to buy Merida Biosciences Inc. for as much as $2.88 billion in cash to expand in autoimmune and allergic diseases, strengthening its immunology portfolio with an experimental treatment for thyroid-related autoimmune conditions.
 
Merida is developing biologics engineered to selectively degrade pathogenic autoantibodies, a precision degradation approach intended to address the biological cause of these diseases rather than broadly suppressing the immune system.
 
Merida's lead program, MER511, is in Phase I development to treat Graves' disease and thyroid eye disease by targeting the autoantibodies that cause them; Graves' disease affects about 1% of the US population and can lead to severe and life-threatening illnesses due to elevated cardiovascular risk.
 [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/lilly-to-buy-merida-for-2-88-billion-for-autoimmune-expansion)


**CRISPR Therapeutics' allogeneic CAR-T shows early promise in autoimmune diseases** — The most surprising area of progress in CRISPR gene editing over the past year may be in autoimmune disease; CRISPR Therapeutics has dosed four participants (two with SLE and two with immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy) in a Phase 1 basket trial using CRISPR-edited allogeneic CAR-T cells targeting CD19, with no severe adverse events reported, and all four patients showing significant clinical improvement at the Day 28 assessment, with one SLE patient maintaining drug-free DORIS clinical remission through Month 6.
 [Life Science Daily News](https://lifesciencedaily.news/crispr-gene-editing-2026/)


**UniQure submits AMT-130 Huntington's gene therapy to FDA and UK MHRA** — UniQure submitted applications to the FDA and the UK's MHRA seeking marketing approval of AMT-130, its treatment for Huntington's disease.
 [STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2026/09/02/biotech-news-arp-h-funds-biotechs-working-on-custom-rna-therapies/)


**EEG-based brain age clock shows intervention sensitivity in longevity trial** — A bioRxiv preprint describes BrainYears, an EEG-based functional brain aging biomarker that predicts chronological age with high accuracy (Pearson r = 0.92; MAE = 4.43 years) and is non-invasive, transportable, cost-effective and suitable for repeated at-home longitudinal measurement; in a longitudinal neuromodulation program, BrainYears-predicted brain age decreased by a mean of −5.18 years in the intervention group whereas a minimal-exposure comparison group showed no change on average (+0.07 years).
 [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.26.714124v1)

## Worth a deeper look

**The autoimmune CAR-T field faces a critical moment.** 
Novartis paused eight autoimmune CAR-T trials after three patient deaths from immune effector cell-associated hemophagocytic syndrome (IEC-HS), a severe systemic inflammatory side effect associated with immunotherapies like CAR-T cell therapy that may be triggered when engineered T cells rapidly expand and activate inside the patient's body.
 
Both Novartis and Bristol Myers are using a rapid manufacturing platform; some have suggested that rapid manufacturing may underlie these reported toxicities.
 
William Blair analysts cautioned that "it is difficult to extrapolate the risk of this side effect to all ongoing CAR-T programs in development for autoimmune diseases," but listed several companies developing CD19 CAR Ts for autoimmune diseases including Autolus, Cabaletta Bio, CRISPR Therapeutics, Kyverna Therapeutics and Allogene Therapeutics.
 The field had been generating excitement with early clinical data showing deep remissions in refractory lupus, inflammatory myopathies, and systemic sclerosis—but these deaths underscore that extending cancer CAR-T platforms to autoimmune settings may carry distinct and incompletely understood risks.
