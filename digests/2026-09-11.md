## ⚛️ Quantum & Deep Tech

**IBM Quantum System Two headed to Switzerland** — 
IBM and Lockheed Martin, in partnership with armasuisse, are establishing a national quantum innovation hub at ETH Zurich, featuring Switzerland's first on-premises IBM Quantum System Two by late 2026
. 
The system, powered by the Nighthawk r2 processor, will be installed at the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre in Lugano
. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**Infleqtion-Cisco quantum networking partnership** — 
Infleqtion and Cisco are partnering to advance distributed neutral-atom quantum networking architectures, combining Infleqtion's cold-atom QPUs and quantum memory modules with Cisco's software-defined networking stack and hardware switches
. 
Their goal is to integrate heterogeneous quantum devices into scalable distributed computing clusters, addressing the need for optical interconnects to scale beyond current quantum processing unit limitations
. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**Altera and Riverlane tackle quantum error correction interfaces** — 
Altera and Riverlane have partnered to optimize quantum error correction interfaces for Altera's Agilex FPGAs, enabling real-time QEC data movement and low-latency control, with an open-source QEC Interface protocol
. 
This initiative complements Altera's recent rollout of Post-Quantum Cryptography support in its Agilex 3 and 5 FPGAs, establishing a dual strategy to secure classical infrastructure against quantum threats and provide control fabric for quantum computing
. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**Chalmers achieves 1,000x speedup in bosonic quantum operations** — 
Researchers at Chalmers University of Technology have developed a new method using Quantum Lattice Gates that accelerates bosonic quantum operations by 1,000x
. The breakthrough addresses a longstanding computational bottleneck in bosonic quantum systems. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**IonQ launches sixth-generation Superion platform** — 
IonQ has unveiled its sixth-generation quantum computing platform, the IonQ Superion 256, featuring Electronic Qubit Control and CMOS integration for high-volume semiconductor fabrication, significantly reducing cost-per-qubit
. 
IonQ aims for commercial deliveries in 2027 and is simultaneously developing the Superion 10K platform for fault-tolerant quantum computing, with pre-orders for the Superion 256 already open
. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**IonQ publishes secp256k1 attack resource estimate** — 
IonQ has published a study detailing a fault-tolerant quantum computing architecture capable of breaking 256-bit elliptic curve cryptography, such as that securing Bitcoin, in 25.7 days
. The end-to-end resource estimate provides concrete numbers for post-quantum cryptography migration planning. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**QuantrolOx expands to UC Berkeley** — 
UC Berkeley's Roger Herst Quantum Nexus has welcomed QuantrolOx, a UK-headquartered qubit automation startup, establishing its first North American base in a five-year MOU that integrates QuantrolOx's Quantum EDGE software with UC Berkeley's quantum processing units to automate qubit tuning and calibration
. 
The collaboration supports California's Quantum California initiative
. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**Universal Quantum opens Singapore R&D hub** — 
Universal Quantum has established its first R&D Competency Centre outside Europe in Singapore, backed by an investment exceeding SGD 30 million
. The expansion marks the company's Asia-Pacific entry. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**AI discovers two new kagome superconductors** — 
Scientists have combined machine learning with quantum physics to discover two new superconductors, YRu3B2 and LuRu3B2, which gain their superconductivity from electrons forming flat bands in a kagome lattice, and demonstrated that AI can rapidly narrow an almost limitless number of possible material combinations to identify the most promising candidates
. 
The approach could dramatically speed the discovery of new superconductors
. [ScienceDaily](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/07/260701205006.htm)

**High-NA EUV production milestone** — 
Intel Foundry and ASML highlighted continued progress in bringing High Numerical Aperture EUV lithography into production, having processed more than one million wafers to date
. 
At the SPIE Photomask Technology + Extreme Ultraviolet Lithography conference, the companies shared updates on reticle stitching, a technique that enables designers to use High NA EUV with today's standard 6-inch masks
. [ASML](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/intel-foundry-and-asml-collaborate-to-accelerate-industry-readiness-for-high-na-euv)

**Imec receives EXE:5200 High-NA system** — 
Imec announced the arrival of the ASML EXE:5200 High NA EUV lithography system, the most advanced lithography tool available today, reinforcing its position as the industry's launchpad into the ångström era
. 
The system will empower imec and its ecosystem partners to unlock the performance needed to pioneer sub-2nm logic and high-density memory technologies
. [Imec](https://www.imec-int.com/en/press/imec-receives-worlds-most-advanced-high-na-euv-system)

**Taiwan-Netherlands photonics collaboration targets 1.6T links** — 
Taiwan's Ligitek Electronics and Liverage Technology are working with ITRI and Dutch photonic chip packaging specialist Phix on a cross-border silicon photonics program targeting phased milestones of 1.6T DR8 by the end of 2026 and 3.2T product development by the end of 2027
. The program addresses AI data center demand for faster optical interconnects. [Digitimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260904PD230/photonics-silicon-2026-data-center-packaging.html)

## Worth a deeper look

**Intel 18A enters production with High-NA EUV** — 
Intel Foundry has entered high-volume manufacturing for a subset of Intel Core Ultra Series 3 processors, code-named Panther Lake, using ASML's EXE High NA EUV technology, with specific Intel 18A layers now dual-qualified on High NA EUV in Oregon and product shipping to customers at yields matched to the NXE platform
. 
This milestone marks an important step in demonstrating High NA EUV readiness in a production environment
. 
The high numerical aperture extreme ultraviolet lithography process is an important next step in EUV lithography, developed by ASML to enable more precise patterning for advanced chip manufacturing
. The deployment demonstrates that High-NA EUV has moved from research tool to production-ready technology, with Intel serving as the first customer to qualify the platform for volume manufacturing. [ASML](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/high-na-euv-reaches-new-readiness-milestone)
