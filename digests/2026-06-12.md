## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Phosphatidylcholine restores mitochondrial youth** — 
Researchers discovered that declining levels of phosphatidylcholine may be a major cause of age-related mitochondrial dysfunction, and boosting this nutrient restored more youthful mitochondrial performance in aging organisms
. The finding suggests some aspects of cellular aging can be slowed or reversed through targeted nutritional intervention. [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/06/260610003119.htm)

**Topical senolytic improves diabetic wound healing** — 
Researchers developed a localized wound dressing carrying ABT-263 and reported that it reduced senescent cell burden, improved healing in diabetic mice, and showed no detectable systemic toxicity
 in 2026 studies. The approach builds on earlier work demonstrating that targeting "zombie cells" can reverse aging in skin. [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/05/260519003215.htm)

**Junevity presents siRNA aging research** — 
The company presented breakthrough data showing that siRNA therapeutics can restore global gene networks to a healthier state, building the case towards human trials of siRNA for age-related and complex diseases
. 
In January 2026, research published in PNAS validated that precise modulation of individual transcription factors can reverse core hallmarks of cellular aging and restore tissue function in vivo
. [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260527230677/en/Junevity-to-Present-Breakthrough-Research-on-siRNA-Therapeutics-at-American-Aging-Associations-2026-Annual-Meeting)

**Proteolytic pathways show neuron-specific aging** — 
A comparative analysis found that while cortical proteasomal activity declined with aging in both cytoplasmic and synaptic neuronal subcompartments, cerebellar proteasomal activity decreased only in the cytoplasmic compartment with aging
, revealing brain region-specific aging patterns. [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.02.729733v1)

**FDA removes two-study requirement for drug approval** — 
FDA officials published a policy article arguing that advances in biology, trial methodology, and statistical science justify moving away from the long-standing expectation of two pivotal trials, stating that over-reliance on two trials "no longer makes sense"
. Both officials expected the change would spur "a surge in drug development." [PharmExec](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/fda-removes-two-study-requirement-new-drug-approval-process-report)

## 🤖 AI

**AI leaders to attend G7 summit** —

AI is expected to be a primary focus of the summit's agenda, with the French G7 presidency soliciting support from the International Energy Agency on finance, energy, and digital issues emphasizing AI's role

**Coding tools converge on agentic blueprint** —

## Worth a deeper look

**The "jagged frontier" and economic uncertainty** — 
In May 2026, Ethan Mollick told corporate leaders at the New York Public Library: "Nobody knows anything. I spend my time talking to AI labs, famous people, I talk to CEOs all the time, and nobody knows anything. We're all making this up as we go along. So anyone who's like, 'We have the playbook' — they're lying to you."
 
Bank of America estimated AI is currently lifting economy-wide productivity per year by just 0.1% — published in the same report that called AI bigger than electricity and the internet combined
. 
The "jagged frontier" of AI advancement, a phrase Mollick popularized, makes predictions truly unstable as all the talk today is based on an understanding of where things are today and assuming that the future resembles the past
. Mollick's framework highlights that the curve's economic payoff is organizational rather than technological — organizations are still learning how to deploy AI effectively at scale. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/25/nobody-knows-anything-this-time-is-different-ethan-mollick-no-playbook-ai/)
