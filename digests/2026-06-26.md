## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Multi-modal longevity intervention achieves measurable bioage reduction** — 
A 17-week pilot trial in 14 adults aged 46-72 tested a program integrating lifestyle optimization, targeted supplementation, and cell-conditioned media infusions; biological aging is a modifiable process, and multi-modal interventions targeting aging hallmarks have shown promise
. The study demonstrates proof-of-concept for combined approaches. [Frontiers in Aging](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/aging/articles/10.3389/fragi.2026.1732426/full)

**Quest Diagnostics' Haystack MRD test wins New York approval** — 
The New York State Department of Health's Clinical Laboratory Evaluation Program approved the Haystack MRD test for identifying residual or recurring disease in solid tumor cancer patients
. This expands access to circulating tumor DNA monitoring outside research settings. [RTTNews](https://www.rttnews.com/corpinfo/fdacalendar.aspx)

**Updated COVID vaccine reduces severe outcomes** — 
A CDC study of more than 111,000 adults found the 2025-26 COVID-19 vaccine provides important protection against severe illness, lowering hospitalization and emergency care visits
. Real-world effectiveness data continues to inform vaccination policy. [RTTNews](https://www.rttnews.com/corpinfo/fdacalendar.aspx)

**Machine learning identifies new gonorrhea therapies** — 
Neisseria gonorrhoeae has evolved resistance to most antibiotics, but a machine-learning screen reveals potential therapies
. The computational approach accelerates identification of drug candidates against resistant pathogens. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/news)

**ADC Therapeutics restructures after safety scare** — 
Following a recent safety scare in a confirmatory trial of Zynlonta, ADC Therapeutics announced layoffs for around 17% of its global workforce
. The antibody-drug conjugate specialist is reorganizing amid regulatory challenges. [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fierce-biotech-layoff-tracker-2026)

## 🤖 AI

**OpenAI ships custom inference silicon with Broadcom** — 
OpenAI and Broadcom unveil LLM-optimized inference chip
, marking OpenAI's entry into purpose-built hardware for model serving. This addresses cost and supply constraints in large-scale inference. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/news/)

**Reasoning model safety: decisions made before visible thinking** — 
Across frontier open-weight reasoning models, the eventual refusal/compliance outcome is strongly predictable from the first token's hidden representation (0.84–0.95 AUROC) before any visible thinking; the intuition that deliberative modes improve alignment may not always be correct
. This challenges assumptions about reasoning transparency. [arXiv](https://scirate.com/)

**EU AI Act transparency rules take effect August 2** — 
Transparency rules of the AI Act come into effect in August 2026; Article 50 introduces transparency requirements for providers and deployers in relation to certain AI system functionalities
. Compliance deadlines are now weeks away for many organizations. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)

**EU lawmakers delay high-risk AI obligations** — 
Following political agreement in May 2026, rules for high-risk AI systems in biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, and employment will apply from December 2027
, pushed back from the original August 2026 date. The delay gives companies more implementation time. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)

**FrontierMath v2 corrects problem set** — 
Epoch released FrontierMath v2 on June 12, 2026 after correcting and removing problematic items
. The revised benchmark aims to provide cleaner evaluation of frontier math capabilities. [LM Council](https://lmcouncil.ai/benchmarks)

**Frontier models gain 30 points on Humanity's Last Exam in one year** — 
Frontier models gained 30 percentage points in a single year on Humanity's Last Exam, a benchmark built to be hard for AI; evaluations intended to be challenging for years are saturated in months
. Benchmark saturation continues to compress useful evaluation windows. [Stanford HAI](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/technical-performance)

## Worth a deeper look

**Claude Tag's enterprise deployment model** — 
Administrators can set limits for token spend both for the organization and for individual channels, and can view a log of everything Claude has done along with who requested each task; for example, a model set up for sales work won't pass on memories to one set up for engineering
. The access-control model addresses data isolation concerns that have slowed enterprise AI adoption, while 
within Anthropic, Claude Tag is already approving and incorporating 65% of code changes the product team submits; Anthropic says Claude Tag's access can be very tightly scoped
. Anthropic is positioning the tool as infrastructure for team-wide AI collaboration rather than individual productivity, targeting the gap between coding assistants and full enterprise deployment.
