## 📈 Finance & Stock Markets

**Semiconductor selloff deepens on AI investment worries** — 
Chipmakers led a tech-sector pullback Wednesday, with Micron plunging 10.6%, AMD losing 6.9%, and Intel dropping 9% as investors questioned whether AI-related capital expenditures have become overstretched
. 
Meta surged 8.8% after announcing plans to develop a cloud business aimed at monetizing excess AI computing capacity, while Microsoft rose 3%, Amazon gained 1.4%, and Alphabet added 1.1%
. [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/stock-market)

**Major indices close mixed as chip rotation continues** — 
The Dow lost 13.96 points or 0.03% to close at 52,305.24, while the S&P 500 dropped 0.22% to 7,483.23 and the Nasdaq declined 0.66% to 26,040.03
. 
The tech-heavy index fell as investors dumped semiconductor names, taking profit after the swath of stocks surged more than 80% in the first half of 2026, with Micron tumbling more than 10% despite being up over 260% year-to-date
. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

**Meta plans cloud infrastructure business to sell AI compute** — 
Meta is developing plans for a cloud infrastructure business that will sell access to AI computing power and models, forming a business to generate revenue from excess computing power sold to outside customers
. 
Meta shares rose more than 10%, easing pressure on a stock that has underperformed the S&P 500 this year with a nearly 15% decline as of Tuesday
. 
Neocloud companies CoreWeave and Nebius fell 10.8% and 12.4% respectively on concerns the move could reduce spending on their services by Meta, a key customer
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/meta-is-building-a-cloud-business-to-sell-excess-ai-compute)

**Getty-Shutterstock merger collapses on UK regulatory demands** — 
Getty Images' board resolved not to pursue divestiture of Shutterstock's editorial business and formally terminated the $3.7 billion merger agreement after the UK Competition and Markets Authority conditioned clearance on selling the editorial unit
. 
Shutterstock fell sharply, off more than 30%, while Getty declined 4%, with stocks tumbling after Getty called off the merger due to demands from the UK regulator
. [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-getty-shutterstock-merger-dead-uk-cma-antitrust-ai/)

**Bending Spoons surges 42% in Nasdaq debut after pricing above range** — 
Bending Spoons, the Italian software company and owner of AOL and Vimeo, saw shares rise 42% in its first trading session after the stock debuted Wednesday on Nasdaq, opening at $31 per share above its IPO price of $29 per share
. 
The Milan-headquartered app giant priced its IPO at $29 per share above the $26-$28 range, raising $1.68 billion from the sale of 57,971,015 ordinary shares with a valuation of approximately $18.4 billion
. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

**Oil prices slide to four-month lows on Iran peace progress** — 
Crude oil fell below $68 per barrel on Thursday, reaching its lowest level since late February as oil shipments through the Strait of Hormuz continued to increase and investors welcomed signs of progress in indirect US-Iran talks, with a US official saying crude flows through the strategic waterway exceeded 10 million barrels per day
. 
Iranian oil exports jumped above 40 million barrels following the lifting of a US naval blockade while record Russian shipments contributed to a significant buildup in seaborne inventories, as President Trump praised the progress in negotiations
. [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/crude-oil)

**June jobs report preview: consensus at 100K-115K payrolls** — 
The Bureau of Labor Statistics will release the June jobs report at 8:30 am Eastern on Thursday, July 2, with economists expecting the US to have added 115,000 new jobs in June and the unemployment rate to have remained 4.3%
. 
Economists expect 100,000 new jobs in June, a nearly 72,000 drop from May according to FactSet consensus estimates, with the unemployment rate forecast to remain unchanged at 4.3%
. [Kiplinger](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/economy/jobs-report-june-2026-what-to-expect)

**Treasury yields hold near 4.5% ahead of jobs data** — 
The yield on the 10-year US Treasury note held around 4.48% on Thursday, maintaining its recent gains as investors cautiously awaited the June jobs report, after data showed private-sector hiring slowed more than expected last month while Fed Chair Kevin Warsh said inflation expectations had eased over the past month
. 
The yield on US 10 Year Note Bond Yield held steady at 4.49% on July 2, 2026
. [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/government-bond-yield)

Nasdaq announced SpaceX qualifies for inclusion in the benchmark technology index, with index-tracking funds and other product sponsors set to begin purchasing shares after the market closes on July 6, with SpaceX officially joining the Nasdaq-100 before trading begins on July 7

SpaceX became one of the quickest additions ever to the Nasdaq-100 index less than a month after the company's blockbuster public debut

**May inflation data: energy drives headline CPI to 4.2% year-over-year** — 
The Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers increased 0.5 percent in May on a seasonally adjusted basis after rising 0.6 percent in April, with the all items index increasing 4.2 percent over the last 12 months before seasonal adjustment, while the index for energy rose 3.9 percent in May and accounted for over sixty percent of the monthly all items increase
. 
The May Consumer Price Index report released on June 10, 2026 showed headline inflation rising at 4.17% year-over-year and at a 8.20% annualized pace over the past three months, with core CPI increasing 2.82% year-over-year and at a 3.17% annualized rate
. The next CPI release is scheduled for July 14 at 8:30 AM Eastern Time, covering June inflation data that will provide critical input for the Fed's July 28-29 policy decision. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm)
