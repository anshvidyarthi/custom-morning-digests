## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Ionis secures first Alexander disease approval two weeks early** — 
The FDA approved Zanvastro (zilganersen) on September 3 as the first disease-modifying treatment for Alexander disease in pediatric and adult patients, an ultra-rare, progressive and often fatal neurological disorder that can affect motor, cognitive, autonomic and gastrointestinal function
. 
The approval came with a priority review voucher, and Ionis now faces its first wholly owned neurology product launch
. 
The RNA-targeted medicine reduces production of glial fibrillary acidic protein (GFAP) and is administered quarterly as a 50 mg intrathecal injection
. [Ionis Pharmaceuticals](https://ir.ionis.com/news-releases/news-release-details/zanvastrotm-zilganersen-approved-fda-first-and-only-disease)

**Vera Therapeutics' kidney drug hits two-year stabilization milestone** — 
TRUTAKNA met all prespecified endpoints in the final efficacy analysis of the ORIGIN 3 trial in 428 adults with primary IgA nephropathy at risk for disease progression
. 
Annualized eGFR decline through 104 weeks was 0.6 versus 5.6 mL/min/1.73m²/year, and kidney-progression risk fell 76%, HR 0.24, p<0.0001
. 
TRUTAKNA received accelerated FDA approval in July 2026, and Vera plans a supplemental BLA submission in Q4 2026
. [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/09/15/vera-kidney-drug-trutakna-shows-strong-phase-3-results-igan/)

**Telix wins first brain imaging PET drug approval** — 
The FDA approved Pixclara (floretyrosine F 18) as the first FET-PET imaging drug for glioma, addressing a significant unmet need by helping physicians differentiate recurrent or progressive glioma from treatment-related change in adults and pediatric patients
. 
FET-PET imaging is recommended in international clinical practice guidelines, including NCCN Guidelines
. 
The approval came three days ahead of its September 11 PDUFA date
. [Telix Pharmaceuticals](https://telixpharma.com/news-views/fda-approves-telixs-brain-cancer-imaging-drug-pixclara/)

**Curium launches first radioligand equivalent for neuroendocrine tumors** — 
The FDA approved Bexlutry (lutetium Lu 177 dotatate) on September 14 for treatment of somatostatin receptor-positive gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumors, including foregut, midgut, and hindgut tumors in adults
. 
Bexlutry is the first radioligand equivalent approved for GEP-NETs and was approved through the FDA's 505(b)(2) pathway with Lutathera serving as the reference product
. 
The product is now available for prescribing physicians
. [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/curium-announces-fda-approval-of-bexlutry-lutetium-lu-177-dotatate-injection-for-adults-with-sstr-positive-gep-nets)

**First photodynamic therapy approved for skin cancer** — 
Biofrontera announced the FDA approved Ameluz red-light photodynamic therapy for superficial basal cell carcinoma, the first FDA-approved photodynamic-therapy drug for a skin cancer
. The approval expands treatment options for non-melanoma skin cancer beyond surgery and topical agents. [Kelin Research](https://kelinresearch.substack.com/p/15092026-biotech-morning-brief-vera)

**Contineum's depression trial misses primary endpoint** — 
Contineum Therapeutics reported Phase 2 MOONLIGHT-1 missed its Day 5 MADRS primary endpoint for JNJ-5120 in major depressive disorder, with no new safety signal
. The setback impacts Johnson & Johnson's licensed program in rapid-acting depression therapies. [Kelin Research](https://kelinresearch.substack.com/p/15092026-biotech-morning-brief-vera)

**Ocular Therapeutix advances wet-AMD filing** — 
A positive FDA pre-NDA meeting kept the AXPAXLI wet-AMD filing on track for Q4 2026 under the 505(b)(2) pathway
. The sustained-release intracameral implant could offer an alternative to frequent anti-VEGF injections. [Kelin Research](https://kelinresearch.substack.com/p/15092026-biotech-morning-brief-vera)

**Neuralink expands to 12 human implants as production scales** — 
As of September, 12 people with severe paralysis worldwide have received Neuralink implants, with patients using them to control digital and physical tools with thought
. 
Elon Musk stated Neuralink will start high-volume production of brain-computer interface devices and move to a streamlined, almost entirely automated surgical procedure in 2026
. 
No BCI is commercially available in 2026, with all current implants performed under research protocols or expanded access programs
. [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/health/elon-musk-shares-plan-mass-produce-brain-implants-paralysis-neurological-disease)

**Synchron prepares pivotal BCI trial with $200M raise** — 
Synchron holds the first FDA IDE for a permanently implanted BCI and is using its $200 million Series D closed November 2025 to fund a pivotal trial in 2026
. 
While Neuralink's device is implanted by removing part of the skull, Synchron's Stentrode is placed via a catheter procedure, with 16 electrodes compared to Neuralink's 1,024, but can still enable severely paralyzed people to control personal devices
. 
In August, Synchron became the first to link its BCI to an iPad and later connected to Amazon's Alexa, allowing a patient to turn on lights, make video calls and play music without using hands, voice or eyes
. [MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/synchron-funding-bci-200m/804977/)

## Worth a deeper look

**AAV gene therapy enters maturation phase amid safety recalibration** — 
Over 180 companies are advancing 250+ AAV-based gene therapy candidates globally, spanning preclinical through marketed stages
. 
The FDA granted approval to Otarmeni, the first dual-AAV gene therapy for OTOF-related genetic hearing loss, representing a significant advancement in sensory disorders
. 
2026 feels like a reset moment for AAV and viral gene therapy: less hype, more hard questions, and a sharper focus on making therapies safer, more targeted, and commercially viable
. 
During the ASGCT annual meeting, conversations increasingly centered on what it will take for AAV platforms to translate strong science into durable clinical and market success, with innovation moving upstream into capsid engineering, tissue-targeted delivery, and machine-learning-guided optimization
. The field confronts manufacturing challenges and safety monitoring as it scales from rare-disease proof-of-concept to broader commercial deployment. [DelveInsight / Aldevron](https://trial.medpath.com/news/aav-gene-therapy-pipeline-surpasses-250-candidates-as-fda-approvals-and-late-stage-trials-accelerate-in-2026)
