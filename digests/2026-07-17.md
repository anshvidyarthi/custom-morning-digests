## ⚛️ Quantum


**NVIDIA unveils open "Ising" decoder family for quantum color codes** — NVIDIA launched an open-source neural-network-driven decoder that reduces logical error rates in triangular color codes by 347x and accelerates runtime by 7.3x compared to existing decoders. The 17-layer 3D CNN, called Ising Decoder ColorCode 1 Fast, acts as a pre-decoder that efficiently handles the computational intensity of color code error syndrome decoding, offering real-time performance and customizable solutions for quantum hardware manufacturers.
 [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)


**Nord Quantique slashes qubit SPAM errors below 0.1% threshold** — Sherbrooke-based Nord Quantique, valued at $1.4 billion, published results demonstrating state preparation and measurement errors below 0.1% on a single-mode grid-state qubit using post-selected stabilization. The breakthrough brings Nord's bosonic platform performance up to speed with hardware from IBM and Google, addressing a fundamental bottleneck that can undermine even sophisticated error-correction protocols.
 [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/nord-quantique-announces-latest-advance-in-quantum-error-correction/)


**Google's Willow learns to self-calibrate in real time using error-correction feedback** — Researchers from Google Quantum AI demonstrated a reinforcement learning system that uses the stream of error-correction data to continuously adjust the Willow superconducting processor's operating parameters while computations run. The technique reduced logical error rates approximately 20% beyond conventional calibration and made performance 3.5 times more stable under artificially introduced hardware drift, published in Nature.
 [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/07/10/google-study-shows-quantum-computer-can-learn-from-its-own-errors-while-it-computes/)


**JPMorgan Chase achieves 95% success on real portfolio optimization with hybrid quantum algorithm** — A research team from JPMorgan Chase and Amazon Advanced Solutions Lab demonstrated a hybrid quantum-classical algorithm, qReduMIS, that reached 95% success probability solving a 225-asset portfolio diversification problem on Quantinuum's 98-qubit Helios trapped-ion system, using real market data from the Nikkei 225. Standalone QAOA failed outright on the problem, but the recursive hybrid approach achieved approximation ratios of at least 0.96, with the arXiv preprint submitted July 1, 2026.
 [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/319845/20260707/hybrid-quantum-algorithm-hits-95-nikkei-225-portfolio-five-quantum-calls.htm)


**Planckian and Quantum Elements partner on superconducting hardware digital twins** — Planckian partnered with Quantum Elements to develop architecture-specific noise models and digital twins for Planckian's superconducting quantum processor. The collaboration uses Quantum Elements' AI-powered Constellation platform to characterize unique noise channels, accelerate quantum error correction decoding, and simulate complex error-syndrome extraction rounds using a Quantum Monte Carlo algorithm, establishing a verified development path toward fault-tolerant computing.
 [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)


**Classiq and ParityQC integrate compiler to tackle qubit connectivity bottleneck** — Quantum software firm Classiq partnered with quantum architecture developer ParityQC, backed by Germany's Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action, to integrate ParityQC's Parity Twine compiler with Classiq's quantum software engineering platform. The collaboration aims to minimize SWAP gates, preserve software portability, and improve performance by optimizing for limited qubit connectivity, a key scalability barrier for running complex algorithms on current hardware.
 [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)


**Photon Queue secures $500K to establish quantum memory operation in New Mexico** — Photon Queue, a UIUC spinout from 2024, secured $500,000 including a $200,000 grant from New Mexico's Quantum Technologies Award program to establish a quantum memory operation in Albuquerque. The company's room-temperature, free-space quantum memories address synchronization challenges in distributed quantum computing, part of a larger $1.2 million state initiative to foster quantum technology and hardware verification.
 [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)


**PsiQuantum appoints two senior executives as utility-scale development accelerates** — PsiQuantum announced Rob Soderbery as Executive Vice President and Sriram Sitaraman as Chief Information Officer as the company scales engineering and operational capabilities. Soderbery will focus on advancing PsiQuantum's silicon photonics platform and scaling engineering operations, while Sitaraman will lead global IT infrastructure, security, and digital transformation; the appointments follow Victor Peng becoming CEO.
 [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/07/16/psiquantum-strengthens-executive-leadership-with-new-appointments/)


**Quantum eMotion's cryptographic module enters NIST testing for FIPS 140-3 validation** — Quantum eMotion's SecureKey Cryptographic Module has been designated "Implementation Under Test" by NIST, a step toward FIPS 140-3 security compliance. The module, integrated with the Sentry-Q platform, uses a "memory-less" cryptographic design to prevent key exposure; the company expects the module to transition to "Modules in Process" status within three to four months, essential for deployments in highly regulated sectors.
 [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)


**AT&T and Palo Alto Networks launch quantum-resilient SASE platform** — AT&T Business and Palo Alto Networks partnered to create a quantum-resilient Secure Access Service Edge (SASE) fabric integrating Palo Alto's Prisma SD-WAN with AT&T's network. The collaboration offers protection against "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" threats using hybrid cryptography, universal control plane hardening, and geofenced telemetry, with automatic updates for new post-quantum cryptography standards and Hybrid PKI securing distributed branch edges.
 [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)


**Post-Quantum's Classic McEliece achieves global ISO standardization** — The Classic McEliece algorithm, developed by Post-Quantum and an international team, achieved global ISO standardization (ISO/IEC 18033-2), becoming the first post-quantum cryptography algorithm to do so.
 [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)


**White House sets 2030/2031 deadlines for federal post-quantum cryptography transition** — President Trump signed Executive Order 14412 on June 22, 2026, setting a December 31, 2030, deadline for federal agencies to transition sensitive systems to post-quantum encryption and a December 31, 2031, deadline for post-quantum authentication. The EO also directs federal contractors to comply with post-quantum FIPS standards by the end of 2030.
 [Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/post-quantum-eo-2026/)

## Worth a deeper look


**Quantinuum, Rolls-Royce, Riverlane, and EPCC team up to accelerate fluid dynamics for gas turbine design** — Quantinuum, Rolls-Royce, Riverlane, and EPCC are collaborating on a multi-year project to advance fluid dynamics simulations for gas turbine design, transitioning from classical emulators to Quantinuum's physical quantum hardware. The initiative integrates hybrid quantum-classical workflows to address computational bottlenecks in fluid dynamics and aligns with the UK's quantum strategy to develop advanced quantum computing systems.
 The project represents one of the first serious attempts to map a high-value industrial simulation workflow onto near-term quantum hardware, testing whether error-corrected trapped-ion systems can deliver engineering accuracy on workloads classical supercomputers struggle with. If successful, the collaboration could establish a validated use case for quantum computers in aerospace and defense R&D before the end of the decade. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)
