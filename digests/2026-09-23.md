**Viking Therapeutics maintenance dosing data reshapes GLP-1 commercialization assumptions** — 
Viking reported that participants maintained up to 90% of weight loss after switching from weekly to monthly injections of VK2735, and 97% with biweekly dosing; patients on monthly dosing retained 85% of week-21 weight loss versus 61% for placebo, while biweekly dosing maintained 90%
. 
At 21 weeks 98% of participants hit 5% weight loss and 90% hit 10%, with an exploratory control cohort reaching 22% weight loss from baseline after 33 weeks of weekly dosing with no plateau observed
. 
Viking shares rose roughly 24% Tuesday morning following the announcement
. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/viking-therapeutics-vk2735-weight-loss-171403809.html)

**Roche enicepatide posts 15.5% weight loss in diabetes cohort, enters late-stage development** — 
Roche's experimental drug enicepatide showed 15.5% average weight loss at the highest dose over 48 weeks in overweight or obese people with type 2 diabetes, met both primary endpoints, and demonstrated a safety profile consistent with similar therapies; Roche plans to launch a late-stage study evaluating cardiovascular outcomes in the first half of 2027
. 
In a separate mid-stage trial reported in June, enicepatide helped patients without diabetes lose 22.7% of their body weight
. 
Analysts peg the global weight-loss drug market at up to $150 billion, and two late-stage obesity studies are already running
. [Reuters / MMM Online](https://www.mmm-online.com/news/five-things-for-pharma-marketers-september-23-2026/)

**Guggenheim upgrades Editas Medicine on cholesterol gene therapy ahead of clinical entry** — 
Guggenheim upgraded Editas Medicine from Neutral to Buy citing optimism around EDIT-401, a gene editing therapy for severe hyperlipidemia; the company plans to initiate a Phase 1/2 clinical trial by the end of 2026 with initial results expected in Q1 2027
. 
A single dose of EDIT-401 achieved 90% or greater mean LDL cholesterol reduction across all dose groups in non-human primates with only moderate levels (10-40%) of functional editing and a six or greater-fold mean increase in hepatic LDLR protein, with durability through approximately six months
. 
Guggenheim's analyst values the therapy with a $5 price target based on a 30% probability of success for EDIT-401 alone
. [GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9093932/edit-looks-405-overvalued-on-gf-value)

**Ultragenyx secures full FDA approval for first Sanfilippo syndrome treatment** — 
The FDA granted standard full approval of Fayuvi (rebisufligene etisparvovec-hopf), an AAV9-based gene therapy for pediatric patients with Sanfilippo syndrome Type A; Ultragenyx said the therapy is the first approved treatment for this condition and the approval was accompanied by a Priority Review Voucher
. 
Updated long-term clinical data represent up to 8 years of follow-up and show further clinical improvement relative to natural history decline, with a durable treatment effect across clinical evaluations and multiple biomarkers
. 
The approval path was not straightforward: the FDA issued a complete response letter in July 2025 citing chemistry, manufacturing, and controls issues
. [Endpoints News](https://endpoints.news/fda-approves-ultragenyxs-gene-therapy-for-sanfilippo-syndrome-type-a/)

**Osteoporosis investment surge follows FDA guidance shift on bone density endpoints** — 
New FDA guidelines on bone density endpoints are driving a surge in venture capital and pharma investment for next-generation osteoporosis treatments; medicines for brittle bones and breakages are grabbing the attention of the pharma industry, which has been incentivized by a long-awaited regulatory change
. 
Osteoporosis used to be a top priority for pharmaceutical companies with Eli Lilly, Merck, Novartis, and GSK each having their own treatment, but cumbersome requirements and safety concerns stifled their use; many patients who are eligible for osteoporosis therapies don't take any of the drugs available today
. [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/09/23/biotech-vc-investors-new-opportunity-osteoporosis-treatments/)

**NewAmsterdam and Menarini win European approval for obicetrapib cholesterol dual therapy** — 
NewAmsterdam Pharma and Menarini received European Commission approval for Ubeslo and Evlarco, the first global approvals for obicetrapib alone and with ezetimibe
. The therapy represents a new approach to lowering LDL cholesterol through CETP inhibition, a mechanism that previously stumbled in clinical development until obicetrapib demonstrated a favorable safety profile. [Kelin Research](https://kelinresearch.substack.com/p/22092026-biotech-morning-brief-vktx)

Johnson & Johnson received European Commission approval for oral IL-23 receptor peptide ICOTYDE in plaque psoriasis

**Merck updates WINREVAIR label with HYPERION data showing 76% clinical worsening reduction** — 
Merck received an FDA label update for WINREVAIR incorporating Phase 3 HYPERION data, where clinical-worsening risk fell 76% versus placebo
. WINREVAIR (sotatercept) is an activin signaling inhibitor for pulmonary arterial hypertension, representing a novel mechanism in a disease with historically limited treatment options. [Kelin Research](https://kelinresearch.substack.com/p/22092026-biotech-morning-brief-vktx)

**PTC Therapeutics completes Sangamo ST-920 Fabry gene therapy acquisition** — 
PTC Therapeutics completed its acquisition of Sangamo's ST-920 Fabry gene-therapy asset; the rolling accelerated-approval BLA remains scheduled for completion in Q4 2026

## Worth a deeper look

**Blackrock Neurotech's BCI applications group scales from research to commercial deployment** — 
At Blackrock Neurotech, Spencer Kellis develops implantable devices that help people with brain and spinal cord conditions communicate and move; Kellis leads a team that develops brain-computer interface tools across software, clinical neuroscience and data science
. 
Researchers at the University of California, Davis recently used Blackrock technology to decode the attempted speech of Casey Harrell, who has ALS, restoring conversational communication using a synthesized version of his own voice; at the Feinstein Institutes for Medical Research, researchers have used it as part of a double neural bypass to help restore movement and sensation following spinal cord injury
. The interview, published September 15 in *The Transmitter*, provides rare operational detail on how academic BCI research translates into commercial product development—a process that remains opaque in most neurotech coverage. [The Transmitter](https://www.thetransmitter.org/frameshift/building-brain-computer-interfaces-at-a-neurotech-company/)
