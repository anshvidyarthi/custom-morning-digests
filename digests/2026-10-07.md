## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Bridge Neurotech exits stealth with ultrasound BCI platform** — 
Bridge Neurotech emerged from stealth October 6 partnering with Butterfly Network to develop a fully non-invasive brain-computer interface using ultrasound, with its first human research study now enrolling participants
. 
The company believes advances in neuroscience, semiconductors and AI have created an opportunity to use ultrasound to image and decode brain activity through the skull
. 
Bridge licensed Butterfly's Ultrasound-on-Chip technology in a multi-year agreement including payments for technology access, development milestones, and hardware purchase commitments
. [Business Wire](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-10-6-bridge-neurotech-and-butterfly-network-announce-partnership-to-develop-fully-non-invasive-brain-computer-interface)

**Vaxcyte's 31-valent pneumococcal vaccine meets all Phase 3 endpoints against Pfizer and Merck** — 
VAX-31 met all prespecified primary endpoints in the OPUS-1 pivotal adult Phase 3 trial, with all 28 VAX-31 serotypes shared with PCV20 and/or PCV21 meeting noninferiority criteria and all three serotypes unique to VAX-31 meeting superiority criteria
. 
The vaccine was well tolerated and demonstrated a safety profile similar to PCV20 and PCV21 across all ages studied
. 
VAX-31 was designed to increase coverage to approximately 95% of invasive pneumococcal disease and approximately 88% of pneumococcal pneumonia circulating in adults in the United States aged 50 and older
. [Vaxcyte SEC Filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001649094/000119312526413168/d21202dex991.htm)

**AbbVie and Genmab's Epkinly posts 51% progression-free survival improvement in first-line lymphoma** — 
Epkinly in combination with R-CHOP demonstrated a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in progression-free survival in newly diagnosed diffuse large B-cell lymphoma patients, reducing the risk of disease progression or death by 51%
. 
This is the first Phase 3 study of a bispecific antibody combination therapy to demonstrate statistically significant improvement in progression-free survival in frontline DLBCL patients
. 
Analysts said the results blew expectations out of the water and could set up a battle with Roche's Polivy
. [AbbVie News](https://news.abbvie.com/2026-10-05-AbbVie-and-Genmab-Announce-Epcoritamab-in-Combination-with-R-CHOP-Demonstrates-Significant-Improvement-in-Progression-Free-Survival-versus-R-CHOP-in-Patients-with-Newly-Diagnosed-Diffuse-Large-B-Cell-Lymphoma)

**Genentech licenses Alector's brain-penetrant Parkinson's enzyme therapy for $1.27 billion** — 
Alector granted Genentech exclusive rights to develop and commercialize AL050, an investigational GCase enzyme replacement therapy enabled by Alector's proprietary blood-brain barrier delivery platform, receiving $100 million upfront with potential for up to $1.17 billion in additional milestone payments
. 
Deficient GCase activity is increasingly recognized as a contributor to Parkinson's disease, most pronounced in carriers of GBA1 mutations, and there is still no approved therapy that addresses the underlying enzyme deficiency
. 
The partnership extends Alector's cash runway into 2029, providing resources to advance its brain-enabled anti-Aβ antibody and siRNA programs
. [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/bridge-neurotech-and-butterfly-network-announce-partnership-to-develop-fully-non-invasive-brain-computer-interface)

**Caribou Biosciences shutters CRISPR CAR-T programs, explores strategic alternatives** — 
Caribou Biosciences is shutting down after failing to raise the money necessary to fund a late-stage clinical trial of its off-the-shelf CAR-T therapy for lymphoma, discontinuing clinical development of its two CRISPR-based CAR-T therapies including vispa-cel
. 
Over the past year, Caribou had worked successfully with the FDA to finalize the design of a Phase 3 trial for vespa-cel, but raising the money to pay for the study proved too difficult, CEO Rachel Haurwitz told STAT
. 
Caribou estimates total restructuring expenses of approximately $15 million to $19 million, including severance and trial wind-down costs
. [STAT News](https://statnews.com/2026/10/07/caribou-biosciences-shutting-down-lymphoma-car-t-therapy)

**Dasatinib-quercetin senolytic combo achieves 47% fibrosis improvement in liver disease trial** — 
In a phase-2, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial, the primary endpoint of ≥1 stage fibrosis improvement without MASH worsening on paired liver biopsies was achieved in 47% of dasatinib plus quercetin-treated participants versus 7% with placebo
. 
MASH resolution occurred more frequently with D + Q than placebo (53% versus 7%), with a greater reduction in NAFLD Activity Score
. 
Thirty-one participants were randomized to receive D + Q (dasatinib 100 mg per day plus quercetin 1,000 mg per day) or placebo for three consecutive days per week over 3 weeks, repeated across three 7-week cycles
. [Nature Metabolism](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42255-026-01643-4)

**CRISPR cholesterol therapy sustains 52.5% LDL reduction for full year** — 
A Phase 1 clinical trial following 15 patients for one year found that participants who received the highest dose of CTX310 had a 52.5% reduction from baseline in LDL cholesterol and a 47.8% drop in triglycerides after 12 months
. 
No serious adverse events related to the therapy were reported during the one-year follow-up
. 
The experimental treatment CTX310 is given as a one-time infusion that carries the CRISPR editing system to the liver, where it switches off a gene known as ANGPTL3
. [Science Daily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260925005434.htm)

**BridgeBio's achondroplasia drug infigratinib granted FDA priority review** — 
The FDA granted priority review to BridgeBio's oral treatment for children with achondroplasia, a common form of dwarfism, with an approval decision expected by Feb. 4, 2027
. The designation accelerates the review timeline for the first potential oral therapy addressing the underlying cause of the most common form of skeletal dysplasia. [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/10/06/biotech-news-astrazeneca-opens-new-boston-site/)

**Novartis commits $7.8B to Chinese mRNA with Abogen licensing deal** — 
Novartis signed a licensing deal worth up to $7.8 billion with Abogen Biosciences, including $575 million upfront and up to $7.2 billion in milestone payments, granting exclusive worldwide rights to ABO2203
. The deal represents one of the largest-ever mRNA licensing agreements and signals Big Pharma's continued belief in the modality beyond COVID-19 vaccines, despite recent setbacks. 
The announcement comes as CSL Limited reported an accounting write-down of approximately $430 million attributable to its mRNA collaboration, citing declining COVID-19 disease burden and onerous U.S. regulatory requirements
, underscoring the commercial uncertainties still facing the mRNA field. [EIN Presswire](https://biotech.einnews.com/)
