## 📈 Finance & Stock Markets

**Fed Raises Rates for First Time in Three Years, Signals More Ahead** — 
The Federal Reserve unanimously raised its benchmark interest rate by 25 basis points to a 3.75%-4% range on Wednesday, the first hike since 2023, with updated projections pointing to the possibility of another rate increase this year
. 
Stocks fell Wednesday, with the Dow seeing its worst day in nearly a month as the 10-year Treasury yield moved back to its highest level since 2007
. 
The dot plot showed that 16 of 18 officials see at least one more 25bps rate hike later this year, with four penciling in two additional increases
. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html)

**Markets Rebound Thursday as Treasury Yields Retreat** — 
Equities sent the S&P 500 up about 1% with chipmakers climbing 3% and Treasury 10-year yields declining from the highest level since 2007, snapping an eight-day rising streak
. 
The benchmark 10-year Treasury yield was more than 5 basis points lower at 4.945%, while the 30-year yield declined more than 5 basis points to 5.296% and the 2-year note slipped more than 4 basis points to 4.681%
. 
Generac jumped 33% in extended trading after Amazon received warrants to purchase up to $340 million worth of its shares under an agreement for Generac to supply backup power generators to Amazon's data centers
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates)

**Generac Surges 40%+ on Amazon Data Center Power Deal** — 
Amazon was granted warrants to purchase up to $340 million worth of Generac stock, with the company issuing warrants to acquire up to 1.69 million shares at $200.93 apiece for a total potential investment worth as much as $8 billion
. 
Initial generator deliveries under the supply agreement are expected to total $2.4 billion across 2027 and 2028
, with 
approximately 308,000 warrant shares becoming exercisable upon signing and the balance unlocking in stages tied to Amazon's generator purchase payments
. 
The arrangement is the latest example of a tech giant striking a warrant-linked deal with a power equipment supplier to keep up with AI-driven electricity demand, following Oracle's similar stake in Bloom Energy in April 2026
. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/amazon-obtains-right-to-buy-up-to-340m-of-generac-boosting-stock-.html)

**Intel, SK Hynix Shares Jump on U.S. Manufacturing Talks** — 
SK Hynix is in talks with Intel to manufacture memory chips in the U.S. for the first time, sending Intel shares up 5% and SK Hynix's Nasdaq-listed shares climbing about 2%
. 
Under one scenario, SK Hynix would lease part of Intel's chipmaking facility in Ohio
, while 
Intel and SK Hynix could also form a joint venture with major cloud firms
. 
SK Hynix said in a statement it is exploring various options to strengthen its global competitiveness but no specific plans or arrangements have been finalized
. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/intel-sk-hynix-us-memory-chips.html)

**Lennar Misses Q3 Estimates as Housing Headwinds Deepen** — 
Lennar reported fiscal third-quarter earnings of $283.9 million, or $1.19 per share ($1.23 adjusted), falling short of the $1.29 analyst consensus
. 
The homebuilder posted revenue of $8.05 billion, which also did not meet Street forecasts of $8.33 billion
. The report comes just hours after 
the Fed raised interest rates for the first time in more than three years, with the move bringing the overnight funds rate to a target range of 3.75%-4%
, further pressuring the housing market. [WUSA9](https://www.wusa9.com/article/syndication/associatedpress/lennar-fiscal-q3-earnings-snapshot/616-2d5ff903-7ed4-4d73-9852-b041cba410ce)

**Oil Prices Pull Back Below $104 on Saudi Supply Workaround** — 
Brent futures traded 2.6% lower at $103.05 per barrel, while West Texas Intermediate crude was last down 1.8% to $100.55
. 
The Saudis are making additional crude cargoes available to Asian refiners through ship-to-ship transfers just outside Hormuz near Oman's Sohar port, with shuttle vessels transporting crude through Hormuz and then loading it onto tankers waiting outside the strait
. 
Saudi Arabia indicated it could restore around half of the damaged East-West pipeline's capacity within days and full operations within six weeks, while also offering additional crude cargoes to Asian refiners through ship-to-ship transfers near Oman
. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/oil-prices-today-wti-brent-hormuz-iran-war.html)

**Tesla Gains 4.3% on Charging Infrastructure Momentum** — 
Tesla shares moved up 4.31% driven by strong buying momentum from operational developments and upbeat institutional sentiment, with a key catalyst being the company's strategic expansion in commercial charging infrastructure through a partnership with Forum Mobility to operate high-power Megacharger stations

High-profile institutional investors publicly reiterated strong bullish conviction, emphasizing accelerating adoption of Full Self-Driving software, solid domestic market share gains, and multi-year upside in AI and robotics
. [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/market-movers/262173410-market-movers-tsla-20260917)

## Worth a deeper look

**The Fed's Hawkish Pivot Reshapes Market Dynamics** — Wednesday's rate hike marked more than just a 25-basis-point adjustment—it represented the first policy tightening in over three years and signaled a fundamental shift in the Fed's inflation fight. 
The Fed unanimously raised the target range for the federal funds rate by 25bps to 3.75%-4.00%, with updated projections showing that 16 of 18 officials see the possibility of at least one more 25bps rate hike later this year, with four penciling in two additional rate increases
. Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference reinforced the hawkish tone, with 
"Inflation remains elevated. Today's policy action will support a timelier return to the Committee's 2 percent goal," the Fed's statement said
. The bond market had already been pricing in this move—
the 10-year Treasury note climbed above 5% on Tuesday, reaching its highest level since July 2007, with yields gaining 29.50 basis points over the past four weeks
. For investors, the key takeaway is that rate cuts—widely expected at the start of 2026—are now off the table, replaced by a tightening cycle driven by 
the Iran war, which has severely restricted the flow of oil through a key Middle East corridor, reducing global energy supplies and raising prices
 alongside persistent inflation in services and sticky wage growth.
