## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Moderna's mRNA flu vaccine wins unanimous FDA panel support** — 
An FDA advisory committee endorsed Moderna's flu vaccine mFlusiva on June 18, 2026, with unanimous 9-0 votes concluding benefits outweigh risks for adults 50-64 and separately for those 65+
. 
The vaccine was found to be about 27% more effective than standard flu shots in late-stage trials

**FDA approves first oral carbapenem for UTIs** — 
FDA approved Utebzi (tebipenem pivoxil) on June 17, 2026, for adults with complicated urinary tract infections including pyelonephritis

**Cognixion launches BCI trial for ALS communication** — 
In March 2026, Cognixion launched a clinical trial in the U.S. to evaluate its integrated wearable device combining augmented reality and BCI, aiming to help individuals with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) communicate more effectively

**BCI field sees remarkable year** — 
2026 BCI advances include BrainGate intracortical typing reaching 22 words per minute with 1.6% error rate, FDA Breakthrough Device Designation for CorTec's stroke rehabilitation system, Motif Neurotech's depression BCI trial approval, and Neuralink's PRIME study approaching enrollment completion with 21+ implanted patients

**Amyloid beta may interfere with tau, not plaques alone** — 
Scientists may have uncovered a hidden trigger behind Alzheimer's disease: instead of plaques being the root cause, amyloid beta appears to interfere with tau, a protein that helps keep neurons functioning properly
. [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/)

**Vietnam accelerates agricultural biotech approvals** — 
The Vietnamese government updated its agricultural biotech regulations via Decree 43/2026/ND-CP, slashing standard approval times from 90 working days to 45 for products already cleared in at least five OECD or G20 nations, aligning with Vietnam's 2025 designation of biotechnology as a strategic pillar for agricultural growth
. [Nature Biotechnology](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03178-1)

## Worth a deeper look

**Moderna's mRNA flu vaccine pivotal moment** — The unanimous 9-0 FDA panel endorsement for Moderna's mFlusiva represents a major milestone in the company's post-pandemic reshaping strategy. 
Advisers concluded benefits outweigh risks for adults 50-64 and separately all voted for ages 65+, with Moderna seeking standard approval for the former group and accelerated approval for the latter
. 
The vaccine was found 27% more effective than standard flu shots in late-stage trials, and because mRNA vaccines can be developed more quickly than traditional flu shots, vaccine makers may be able to update them later in the year to better match circulating strains
. This could reshape the $6 billion annual influenza vaccine market if the FDA follows the panel's recommendation.

**The US-China AI parity moment** — June 2026 marks an inflection point where Chinese AI labs achieved functional parity with Western frontier models while dramatically undercutting on price. 
Alibaba's Qwen 3.7 Max firmly cemented itself in the top tier (56.6% overall index) at just $3.75 per 1M tokens, heavily undercutting the $11+ pricing of top Western models
. 
MiniMax 3 secured a 1528 Elo at $0.53 per 1M tokens, making it an astonishingly cheap multimodal powerhouse leading the market in voice agents and high-EQ AI
. 
Inference costs are falling roughly 10x per year for the same level of performance, with GPT-4-level capability costing about $30 per million tokens in early 2023 and available for under $1 per million tokens today
. This cost collapse is forcing Western labs to compete on capability rather than just availability.
