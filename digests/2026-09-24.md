**Treasury Yields Surge to Multi-Decade Highs as Inflation Concerns Mount** — 
The 10-year Treasury yield held around 5.11% Thursday, near its highest level since July 2007
, while 
the 30-year yield rose to 5.43%, its highest level since 2004
. 
The rise accelerated following a weak $70 billion auction of five-year notes, and S&P Global data showing US private-sector activity expanded at its fastest pace in more than five years in September with stronger inflationary pressures
. 
Markets are now pricing in around a 70% chance of a Fed rate hike in October, up from 55% a day earlier
. [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/government-bond-yield)

**Oracle Invokes Force Majeure on Massive AI Data Center, Stock Sinks** — 
Oracle sent a force majeure notice to Blue Owl Capital regarding its Project Jupiter data center in New Mexico, attempting to defer payments should the facility fail to come online in 2028 as planned
. 
Oracle shares fell 4% Thursday following the news
. 
The debt tied to the development is already trading at stressed levels, below 90 cents on the dollar, according to sources, with a group of about 20 banks having provided an $18 billion loan to fund construction
. 
The project was designed for 2.45 gigawatts of electricity and faced denial of a key permit for energy resources
. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-24/oracle-cites-force-majeure-to-shield-itself-on-controversial-data-center)

**Darden Restaurants Misses Earnings as Olive Garden Struggles** — 
Darden reported fiscal first-quarter net income fell to $233.4 million ($2.04 per share) from $257.8 million ($2.19 per share) a year earlier, with total sales rising 5.1% to $3.2 billion
. 
The quarterly results narrowly missed analyst expectations of $2.06 EPS and $3.21 billion revenue, with companywide same-store sales of 3.1% falling short of expectations for 3.3% growth
. 
LongHorn Steakhouse recorded a 6.2% comparable-sales gain, whereas Olive Garden managed only 1.1% growth
. 
Shares fell as much as 5% in premarket trading but pared back losses to about 2% in morning trading
. [Qz](https://qz.com/darden-restaurants-earnings-profit-olive-garden-same-store-sales-092426)

**Starbucks Announces Second Wave of Store Closures Under Turnaround** — 
Starbucks is closing approximately 250 locations in North America, amounting to 1% of its roughly 18,000 location footprint
. 
The affected locations didn't "deliver the experience we want for customers and partners" or aren't financially viable, the company said
. 
The company expects to incur about $300 million in restructuring charges related to the closures, with about $200 million related to costs of exiting leases early and paying employees separation benefits
. 
CEO Brian Niccol announced a similar amount of closures a year ago as part of a $1 billion restructuring effort
. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/24/food/starbucks-closures)

## Worth a deeper look

**The Oracle Force Majeure and AI Infrastructure Financing Risk** — Oracle's force majeure notice on Project Jupiter represents more than a single company's struggle with a troubled development—it may signal broader stress in the AI infrastructure financing boom. 
A group of about 20 banks provided an $18 billion loan to help fund construction, and the notice raises questions about the resilience of data center leases, as contracts often include clauses allowing customers to back out if service doesn't begin as scheduled
. 
Force majeure clauses are becoming more common in data center developments, with a Quinn Emanuel client alert noting "in AI data center projects, force majeure is no longer a back-end boilerplate provision—it is a core litigation and risk-allocation tool that can determine whether a delay remains isolated — or cascades through the project's construction, customer, power, insurance, and financing documents"
. 
Project debt financing is already trading below par at less than 90 cents on the dollar
, suggesting lenders are bracing for potential losses even as Oracle insists the project remains on schedule.
