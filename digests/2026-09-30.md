## 🧬 Biotech / Longevity / Neurotech

**Zealand Pharma's petrelintide posts double-digit weight loss with minimal GI side effects** — 
Zealand's amylin analog achieved 7.9-9.8% weight loss at 28 weeks versus 1.7% with placebo, increasing to 8.7-10.7% at week 42, with 34-56% of patients losing at least 10% of body weight
. 
Around 20% of patients experienced mild nausea, with less than 7% experiencing vomiting, diarrhea or constipation—far lower than the 50%+ gastric adverse event rates typical with GLP-1 agonists
. 
Roche has initiated three Phase 3 trials targeting obesity/overweight, obesity plus type 2 diabetes, and obesity plus cardiovascular disease
. [The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology / Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/roche-zealand-see-phase-2-amylin-weight-loss-peak-mid-dose-start-pivotal-program)

**UniQure's Huntington's gene therapy shows durability concerns at four-year mark** — 
AMT-130 slowed Huntington's disease progression by 44% compared to external controls after four years, but the difference was not statistically significant
. 
The FDA reversed its position multiple times over the past year on whether the existing Phase 1/2 dataset was sufficient to support a BLA, before ultimately agreeing in June 2026 that the three-year analysis could serve as primary evidence
. 
The company submitted both a BLA and MAA on September 2, 2026
. The data suggest the therapy's effect may be weakening over time compared to the 75% reduction seen at three years. [STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/09/29/uniqure-huntingtons-gene-therapy-slows-disease-progression-four-years/)

**Paradromics participant becomes first to use Connexus BCI for communication** — 
In June 2026, a woman with motor neuron disease became the world's first person to use the Connexus brain-computer interface; this month she communicated using the implant itself, saying "Okay, I have a lot to say"
. 
Paradromics' efforts represent a real-time communication breakthrough in a commercial product rather than a research system
. 
The surgical placement was led by University of Michigan neurosurgeon Matthew Willsey, who demonstrated Connexus can be safely implanted, record electrical brain signals, and be removed intact in less than 20 minutes
. [Forbes / Yahoo Finance](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/personal-paradromics-paces-landmark-implanted-212011359.html)

**Moderna-Merck mRNA cancer vaccine succeeds in Phase 3 melanoma trial** — 
Intismeran combined with pembrolizumab as adjuvant therapy produced statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvements in recurrence-free survival and distant metastasis-free survival compared with pembrolizumab alone in patients with completely resected stage IIB-IV melanoma
. 
The Phase 3 trial met its primary goal of recurrence-free survival and a secondary endpoint of distant metastasis-free survival; earlier Phase 2 data showed the combination reduced the risk of recurrence and distant metastasis by 49% compared with Keytruda alone
. This marks the first late-stage success for a personalized neoantigen mRNA vaccine. [C&EN / AJMC](https://cen.acs.org/pharmaceuticals/vaccines/mrna-cancer-vaccine-succeeds-clinical-trial/104/web/2026/08)

**Columbia unveils ultra-miniaturized BCI chip with 100x data bandwidth** — 
BISC (Biological Interface System to Cortex) is a single silicon chip thinned to 50 μm with a total volume of approximately 3mm³, integrating 65,536 electrodes, 1,024 simultaneous recording channels, and 16,384 stimulation channels
. 
The external relay station provides power and data communication through a custom ultrawideband radio link that reaches 100 Mbps, a throughput at least 100 times higher than any other wireless BCI currently available
. 
Youngerman, Shepard, and NewYork-Presbyterian were recently awarded an NIH grant to implement BISC in drug-resistant epilepsy management
. [Nature Electronics / Columbia Engineering](https://www.engineering.columbia.edu/about/news/silicon-chips-brain-researchers-announce-new-generation-brain-computer-interface)

**CAR-T expansion into autoimmune disease reaches Phase 3** — 
Multiple early studies have demonstrated that a single CD19 CAR-T infusion can induce sustained, drug-free remission in treatment-refractory SLE, myasthenia gravis, and inflammatory myopathies; Kyverna Therapeutics and Cartesian Therapeutics are the furthest along in pivotal trials
. 
If Phase 3 data confirm Phase 1/2 results, CAR-T could enter the autoimmune market—a far larger commercial opportunity than hematologic malignancies
. 
As of September 2026, 936 CAR-T cell therapy trials are active with 329 sponsors and 18 in Phase 3
. [DataLookout](https://datalookout.com/car-t-cell-therapy-clinical-trials)

**CorTec completes first German-made BCI implant in stroke patient** — 
CorTec's Brain Interchange BCI was implanted in late July at Harborview Medical Center under FDA IDE; the trial will gather initial safety data and evaluate whether direct cortical electrical stimulation can enhance upper-limb motor recovery in stroke patients
. 
The implant system continuously records brain activity, instantly interpreting signals and delivering targeted electrical stimulation in real time to enhance neuroplasticity
. 
The first implantation marks a milestone for European medical neurotechnology and underscores CorTec's emergence as Germany's first implantable BCI developer ready to compete on the global stage
. [CorTec](https://cortec-neuro.com/first-human-implantation-of-a-bci-made-in-germany/)

**CRISPR field enters personalized medicine era with FDA framework** — 
Researchers at Children's Hospital of Philadelphia and Penn Medicine designed and delivered a bespoke base-editing therapy to a patient named Muldoon, with the first infusion in February 2025 when he was six to seven months old, making him the first person to receive a personalized CRISPR-based gene therapy; as of early 2026, he is walking and showing only mild symptoms
. 
In February 2026, the FDA published draft guidance introducing the "plausible mechanism framework," a pathway designed specifically for individualized genomic therapies targeting ultra-rare diseases that allows a single clinical trial to test a platform technology customized for each patient
. 
More than 150 CRISPR-related trials are now active globally according to the Innovative Genomics Institute's March 2026 update
. [Life Science Daily News](https://lifesciencedaily.news/crispr-gene-editing-2026/)

## Worth a deeper look

**BCI investment surge signals commercial inflection point** — 
In 2026, global investment in BCI and neurotech surpassed $1 billion, led by US players like Neuralink, Synchron, Blackrock Neurotech, and Precision Neuroscience, while China designates BCIs as a strategic sector and backs "world-class" champions with state funds and fast-tracked trials
. 
Most capital is flowing into invasive or endovascular implants that offer higher-quality signals, even as non-invasive EEG/MEG systems improve with AI decoding and remain attractive for research and rehabilitation
. 
Science's regulatory approval marks the second commercial BCI to hit the market this year, following Neuracle's approval from China's NMPA; Neuracle's NEO system involves a coin-sized wireless implant that sits on the brain's outer membrane and controls a robotic glove, specifically designed for people with spinal cord injuries
. The shift from research prototypes to commercial products—with multiple companies now in pivotal trials—suggests 2026-2027 could mark the beginning of routine clinical BCI deployment for paralysis, ALS, and stroke rehabilitation.
