## ⚛️ Quantum & Deep Tech

**DOE Launches $215M Fault-Tolerant Quantum Competition** — 
The U.S. Department of Energy announced the Quantum Genesis Q Competition with up to $215 million in planned funding to demonstrate the world's first fault-tolerant scientifically relevant quantum computers, requiring at least 100 logical qubits capable of hundreds of millions of fault-tolerant operations
. 
Phase II includes a $100 million general incentive pool plus two $50 million bonus pools for teams demonstrating 150 and 200 logical qubits
. 
A parallel $45 million Quantum High-Performance Computing Validation and Verification Testbed Lab Call will fund National Laboratories to rigorously characterize and validate quantum systems
, with applications due October 19. [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/science/articles/doe-launches-competition-accelerate-development-worlds-first-fault-tolerant)

**IonQ-Synopsys Quantum Acceleration Wins IEEE Best Paper** — 
IonQ, Synopsys, and NVIDIA demonstrated quantum-accelerated Computer-Aided Engineering workflows reducing execution time by up to 14.6%, with research awarded 1st Place Best Paper at IEEE Quantum Week 2026 using a quantum matrix reordering algorithm embedded in Ansys LS-DYNA
. 
The work tested models with meshes of up to 35 million data points using simulations of up to 150 qubits, physically validated on IonQ's 36-qubit Forte system
. This marks one of the first real-world industrial engineering speedups from NISQ-era hardware. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**Harvard Extends Qubit Coherence Using Sound Waves** — 
Harvard researchers demonstrated protection of quantum information using microscopic sound waves, extending diamond-based qubit coherence time by roughly threefold by continuously surrounding qubits with mechanical vibrations
. 
Instead of microwave pulses, the team continuously applied a mechanical driving field of phonons, shifting a silicon-vacancy spin into a "dressed" qubit state that is significantly less vulnerable to low-frequency environmental disruptions
. 
The same phonons could eventually both transmit and protect quantum information, opening the door to compact sound-based quantum networks on chips
. [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260911214245.htm)

**Oxford Quantum Circuits Releases Erasure Noise Simulator** — 
Oxford Quantum Circuits launched Erado, an open-source Qiskit simulator for erasure noise and post-selection integrated into their QCaaS SDK, with research showing postselection can fully mitigate erasure noise below 3.0% error rates, outperforming single-rail architectures
. 
Erado offers flexible simulation modes and precise parameter controls for analyzing the trade-offs of post-selection
. The tool addresses a critical gap in modeling erasure-aware error mitigation for dual-rail qubit architectures. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**IQM Expands to South America with First Private-Sector Sale** — 
IQM Quantum Computers is selling an on-premises IQM Spark system to Brazil's Eldorado Research Institute for deployment in Q1 2027, marking the first quantum computer acquisition by a private research institution in Brazil, combined with cloud access to IQM's European 54-qubit QPU
. 
The system will enable Eldorado to collaborate with universities, startups, and industrial partners to develop quantum algorithms and explore hybrid HPC-AI-quantum workflows
. This reflects the geographic diversification of quantum hardware sales beyond North America and Europe. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**Q-SAFE Launches EU Carrier-Grade Quantum-Safe Network** — 
Q-SAFE Solutions launched a carrier-grade quantum-safe network platform in the EU-27 integrating Quantum Key Distribution and Post-Quantum Cryptography with centralized hybrid key management, operating over existing fiber-optic infrastructure without requiring dedicated dark fiber
. The platform is designed for telecommunications providers, critical infrastructure, and government agencies preparing for post-quantum cryptographic transitions. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**Alice & Bob Integrates Cat Qubits into HPC Workflows** — 
Alice & Bob is partnering with CEA to integrate its cat qubit software into High-Performance Computing environments, aiming to develop hybrid supercomputing workflows and algorithms for fault-tolerant quantum computing
. Cat qubits—which encode quantum information in oscillator states—offer intrinsic protection against bit-flip errors, potentially reducing the overhead required for error correction at scale. [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/news/)

**Tower Semiconductor Highlights Silicon Photonics for AI and Quantum** — 
Tower Semiconductor announced participation in ECOC 2026 (September 21-23, Málaga) showcasing its high-volume silicon photonics platform for datacenter and telecom optical transceivers, near- and co-packaged optics for AI cluster architectures, DWDM lasers, optical circuit switching, FMCW LiDAR, and quantum computing
. 
The company will also feature Silicon Germanium BiCMOS solutions addressing higher-bandwidth, lower-latency, and lower-power demands
. [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/15/3361818/0/en/tower-semiconductor-to-highlight-its-high-volume-silicon-photonics-and-sige-solutions-for-ai-infrastructure-telecom-and-emerging-applications-at-ecoc-2026.html)

**TSMC 2nm Production Ramps Sharply in Q3** — 
TSMC's 2-nanometer process accounted for 3% of Q2 wafer revenue, with advanced technologies (7nm and below) comprising 77% of total revenue; management expects steep 2nm ramp-up in Q3 driven by strong demand for leading-edge process technologies
. 
By mid-2027, TSMC's 2nm monthly output is expected to climb roughly 22% from 90,000 to 110,000 wafers, while 3nm production plans to increase from 180,000 by end of 2026 to 210,000 by mid-2027
. The surge reflects AI accelerator demand and the shift from mobile-first to HPC-first adoption of advanced nodes. [TSMC SEC Filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001046179/000104617926000451/a2q26e_withguidancexfinal.htm)

**NIF Achieves 11th Ignition Shot at 7.9 MJ Yield** — 
The National Ignition Facility achieved ignition for an 11th time on June 20, 2026, with yield measured at 7.9 MJ and a target gain of approximately 3.8
. 
Additional experiments using higher laser energies and producing even higher energy yields are expected, further demonstrating that NIF can repeatedly conduct fusion experiments at multi-megajoule levels of energy output
. Repeatability remains the critical transition from physics demonstration to engineering validation for inertial confinement fusion. [LLNL](https://lasers.llnl.gov/science/achieving-fusion-ignition)

## Worth a deeper look

**Quantum Error Correction Becomes Industry's Central Challenge** — The shift from NISQ to fault-tolerant quantum computing is accelerating faster than hardware roadmaps suggested even 18 months ago. 
Real-time quantum error correction has become the industry's defining engineering hurdle, with hardware platforms across trapped-ion, neutral-atom, and superconducting technologies crossing error-correction thresholds and shifting focus toward decoding hardware, system integration, and classical bandwidth limits
. 
The number of firms treating error correction as a differentiator doubled since last year, with multiple hardware platforms crossing the performance threshold needed for error correction in the past 12 months
. The DOE's $215 million Quantum Genesis Q competition codifies this pivot: funding is contingent not on qubit count but on demonstrating 100+ logical qubits executing hundreds of millions of fault-tolerant operations—a standard that only a handful of groups worldwide are positioned to meet by 2028. Meanwhile, practical deployment faces significant obstacles including talent shortages, rising system complexity, and the need to validate architectures against measurable performance and cost targets. [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2025/11/19/quantum-report-says-error-correction-now-the-industrys-defining-challenge/)
